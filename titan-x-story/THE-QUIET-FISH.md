# The Quiet Fish Who Waited in the High Dark

A story for listening.  
Prime Field Technologies LLC · TITAN-X  
Read this aloud. Then send the numbers PDF if they ask.

---

Once, the fire came first  
and the help came after.

The help came in a hurry.  
The help came too late.

The airplanes were brave.  
The airplanes were few.  
The airplanes could not stay.

So a quiet fish was made  
to live in the high dark.

Not a fish of the sea.  
A fish of the sky.

It was wide, like a manta.  
It was light, like a held breath.  
The sun fed it.  
The quiet air held it up.

It did not scramble.  
It waited.

It waited over the trees  
while the trees were still green.

When the fire came,  
the quiet fish was already there.

It could watch.  
It could carry.  
It could drop what the ground needed  
and stay.

One fish.  
Then another.  
Twelve, if the years are kind.

California first —  
because that is where the story is already burning.

A house on the ground still matters.  
A porch still matters.  
But some listening has to happen  
up where the weather is thin  
and the night is long.

That is TITAN-X.

It is not a shout.  
It is a wait.
