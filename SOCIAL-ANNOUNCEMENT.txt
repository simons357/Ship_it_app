Introducing TITAN-X.

Prime Field Technologies LLC is announcing TITAN-X — a persistent stratospheric aerial platform built to stay on station, not scramble after the fact.

It is a free-flight aerostat: swept manta hull, helium buoyancy, solar power, indefinite endurance, 20,000 lb modular payload. Altitude band 500–130,000 ft. One hull family. Modules swap.

First concentration: California firefighting. Pre-positioned coverage turns response from days into hours — retardant, water, sensors, and incident command on the same platform. Metro disaster and infrastructure nodes follow.

Planning base through 2030: 12 units. Unit CAPEX $13M. Fleet CAPEX $156M. OPEX $2.4M per unit per year.
