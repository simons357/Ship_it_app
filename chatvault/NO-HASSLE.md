# No-hassle policy

Owner: Jonathan Robert Simons. Date locked: 12 September 2026.  
His words: “I don’t want people hassling me.”

This is a product constraint, not a preference.

## What that means

- ChatVault is a **personal vault** until he explicitly asks to sell it.
- **No App Store listing.** Apple Developer membership is for later private signing, not a public page where strangers can review or email him.
- **No support inbox** in the app, on a store page, or on a landing page.
- **No Stripe / no customers** until he wants customers. Paying users are hassle by definition.
- **Do not circulate** `preview--6a58e103fedcde66a0a7710e.base44.app`. That host is unauthenticated and world-writable. Anyone with the link can write into it.
- **Do not send** the Base44 editor URL. It freezes on a phone and is a login wall.
- Local engine (`chatvault/`) stays on-device. No accounts. No chat. No comments.

## What this agent will not do

Open a public listing, add a contact form, add analytics that phones home, wrap Base44, submit to App Review, or put an email on a store or landing page.
