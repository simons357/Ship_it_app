# Coherence — locked definition (Prime Field)

Use this wording everywhere. Do not dilute it into “vibes,” “harmony,” or generic AI fluff.

---

## One-sentence lock

**Coherence** = measurable alignment among coupled parts of a system over time — high when those parts stay phase-consistent and jointly stable; low when they drift, disperse, or fight each other.

---

## What it is (and is not)

| Coherence **is** | Coherence **is not** |
| --- | --- |
| An **index of alignment** across named parts (vessels + routes + environment; assets + signals; operator + key) | A mood word for “things feel good” |
| Something you can **score, trend, and alert on** | Synonymous with “harmony,” “resonance,” or “beauty” alone |
| A **leading indicator** of instability (drift shows up before failure) | A claim that the future is known |
| Domain-agnostic math applied to ops (maritime, finance, presence) | Owned by unrelated Hormuz / UCMS papers — those are not yours |

**Resonance** = the *mechanism / field language* (why coupling locks).  
**Coherence** = the *measurable state* of that coupling (how locked it is right now).

## What can be measured

These are the legitimate measurable faces of coherence. Prefer this list in decks and demos.

### A. Operational coherence (maritime, finance, logistics)

| Measurable | Plain meaning | How it shows up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Coherence index** \(C \in [0,1]\) or 0–100 | How aligned the coupled parts are *now* | Single score on the dashboard |
| **Component contributions** | Which part (vessel / route / weather / signal) is helping or hurting \(C\) | Breakdown / bars |
| **Decoherence rate** \(\mathrm{d}C/\mathrm{d}t\) | How fast alignment is falling apart | Trend / sparkline |
| **Time-to-instability** | Estimated lead time before \(C\) crosses a risk threshold | Alert / countdown |
| **Cross-lag / phase offset** | Whether two series move together or one leads | Lag plot / phase |
| **Scenario delta** \(\Delta C\) → \(\Delta \$\) | If we change a variable, how coherence and money move | Harmonic Insights / finance scenarios |
| **Threshold breach count** | How often \(C\) dipped below an agreed floor | Ops KPI |

Maritime public framing you already use: *live insight into emerging instability before it becomes operational risk* — that is decoherence detection + lead time, not mysticism.

### B. Presence coherence (Field Lock)

| Measurable | Plain meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Bound vs unbound** | Is the key currently presence-locked to the rightful party? |
| **Presence continuity** | Duration / integrity of the bind without dropouts |
| **Unauthorized attempt rate** | Failed binds or spoof attempts while locked |
| **Rebind latency** | Time to restore a valid presence bind after interruption |

Here coherence = **human–machine alignment that stays true**. The measurable is presence integrity, not a vibes score.

### C. Spectral / fluid coherence (research spine — black box on methods)

| Measurable (public-facing names) | Plain meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Energy concentration vs dispersal** | Is energy staying in structured modes or spraying into noise? |
| **Triad / cascade balance** | Are interacting scales staying orderly over time? |
| **Regularity / non-blowup indicators** | Conditional signals that the field stays controlled |

Keep equations and recipes off the public site. Partners get: *we can score concentration vs dispersal*; detail under NDA / papers.
