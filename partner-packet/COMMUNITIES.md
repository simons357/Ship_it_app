# Communities — where to show up  
### Seriousness: ★★★★☆ (strategy) / ★★★★★ (math rooms)

Joining communities is smart for exposure.  
**Join rooms that match the work** — not every room with a pulse.

---

## Plain and simple rule

| Room vibe | Fit for you? |
| --- | --- |
| Crystal gazing / pure woo | Weak for SFE / RH / demos credibility |
| Operators, builders, applied math, defense/tech, founders | Strong |
| “Manifest abundance” with no demos | Skip or lurk only |
| People who click live links and ask hard questions | Stay |

Your law still holds: **no bullshit at the moment of action.**  
If the community can’t handle a demo link and a two-word answer (“I manage complexity”), it’s the wrong room.

### Operator / clinical-adjacent (careful)
- Anesthesia / human-factors / simulation communities — share *operator systems* thinking, not unapproved device claims  

### Skool specifically
Skool is a **tool**, not a strategy.  
- Keep groups that are plain, builder, or operator-minded  
- Exit or mute groups that are only crystal gazing if they dilute your face  
- One Skool presence max until it pays in real conversations

## About “I can write 10 papers a day”

Capability ≠ strategy.

| Fast drafting | What serious rooms reward |
| --- | --- |
| Volume | Clarity, labeled claims, revisable public record |
| AI-assisted speed | One sharp object people can open |
| Many titles | A few papers + a demo that uses the spine |

**Law for publishing pace:**  
Ship **quality-labeled** work (proved / conditional / open).  
Prefer **one paper + one demo** in a community over ten PDFs nobody reads.  
Math is out there; most people don’t care — so make the **demo** the handshake.
