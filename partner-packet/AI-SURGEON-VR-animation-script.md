# AI Surgeon VR — Animation Script (v1)

**Source:** Jonathan Simons / Simons Medical Innovations — LinkedIn project *Surgeon X* + ProVR Tools notes  
**Use:** Paste into a script-to-animation / text-to-video app  
**Length target:** ~60–90 seconds (trim scenes as needed)  
**Tone:** Competitive training game that happens to teach real skill — not a hospital ad

---

## LOG LINE (for the tool’s “prompt” box if needed)

A young player boots into a VR operating theater. An AI Attending coaches in real time. Haptic tools bite like metal. Team surgery turns practice into a tournament. Gaming skill becomes medical readiness.

---

## SCRIPT

### SCENE 1 — BLACK → BOOT
**Visual:** Black screen. Heart-monitor beep once. VR headset lights ignite. HUD boots: `AI SURGEON VR`.  
**On-screen text:** AI SURGEON VR  
**VO (calm, confident):**  
The OR used to be somewhere you only watched.

**SFX:** Soft boot-up. Distant OR ambient.

---

### SCENE 2 — FIRST PERSON IN THEATER
**Visual:** First-person in a clean virtual OR. Patient draped. Tray of instruments. Player’s hands raise — Bluetooth haptic tools (scalpel / clamp silhouette) lock into tracking.  
**On-screen text:** ProVR Tools · haptic feedback  
**VO:**  
Now you step in — feel the weight, the balance, the cut.

**Action:** Tool vibrates subtly on contact with tissue plane. AI reticles mark the safe corridor.

---

### SCENE 3 — AI ATTENDING
**Visual:** Soft holographic attending figure (or voice-only HUD coach) appears at shoulder. Score rings: accuracy · economy of motion · time.  
**On-screen text:** AI Attendings™ · adaptive coaching  
**AI ATTENDING (VO):**  
Angle down two degrees. You’re rushing the field. Breathe. Again.

**VO (narrator):**  
An AI attending that coaches, corrects, and certifies — in real time.

**Action:** Player adjusts. Green check. Difficulty ticks up automatically.

---

### SCENE 4 — MULTIPLAYER TEAM SURGERY
**Visual:** Split / shared theater — two or three players: primary, assist, scrub. Shared timer. Tournament bracket flashes in corner.  
**On-screen text:** Team Surgery Mode · medical esports  
**VO:**  
Solo reps. Then teams. Then tournaments.

**Action:** Clean handoff of an instrument. Crowd/remote viewer count ticks up. Bracket advances one round.

---

### SCENE 5 — PROGRESSION / PATHWAY
**Visual:** Montage: skill tree unlocks · badge certs · scholarship / sponsorship pathway icons (keep abstract; no fake university logos).  
**On-screen text:** Train · Compete · Progress  
**VO:**  
Not just high scores — a path from game skill toward real capability.

---

### SCENE 6 — CLOSE
**Visual:** Pull back from OR HUD to silhouette of player removing headset, still focused. Logo lockup.  
**On-screen text:**  
AI SURGEON VR  
Simons Medical Innovations  
**VO:**  
The world’s first competitive medical-esports platform.  
Where practice feels real — and the next generation trains before they ever scrub in.

**End card:**  
js@primefieldtechnologies.com
