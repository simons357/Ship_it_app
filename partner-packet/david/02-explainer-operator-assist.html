Operator Assist · Wave mode

Emergency decision support for the anesthesia operator — suggestions in seconds, never a takeover.

The problem

At the head of the table, cognitive load spikes when the room tilts: alarms, lagging numbers, conflicting trends, and not enough clear, do-able next moves. Most “AI for anesthesia” dumps more data. That raises load.

The product idea

Wave mode does not ask to take over. It finds the most likely, do-able candidates, stress-tests them fast (many simulated futures), keeps the survivors with the highest success share against a named goal, and shows them as visuals — so you can choose in seconds. Built for emergency decision-making by design.

1 · StateWhere are we now?
2 · CandidatesOnly what’s do-able in this room
3 · Stress-testRun futures hard; keep survivors
4 · You chooseVisual menu — human owns the call

What it is

  • Decision assist — not closed-loop control
  • Short menu (few options), not a dashboard novel
  • Ready glass stays quiet; Wave mode sheds noise
  • Success % tied to a named goal + assumptions
  • Custom / “not sure” always allowed

What it is not

  • Not “the computer runs the case”
  • Not a cleared patient monitor (yet)
  • Not quantum-computer marketing
  • Not Millennium / prize math on the glass
  • Not guaranteed outcomes — sim-conditioned

How it fits the company

Same spine as the OR: determine state → fast loop → custom layers → emergency mode → decision options. Vigilant (pre-recognition / trajectory cues) and Reflex Staircase (crisis ladder / teaching) can feed Wave mode — they don’t replace the human.
