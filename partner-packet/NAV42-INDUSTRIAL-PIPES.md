## Financials — make the beam efficient for refiners

Refineries buy **energy and downtime**, not “lattice poetry.”

| Lever | How to talk money |
| --- | --- |
| **Pump / compression energy** | Lower effective friction → less kWh per barrel moved (scenario delta → $) |
| **Throughput** | Same pipe, more stable flow regime → capacity without new steel |
| **Turnaround coating window** | Apply in planned outage — shared costs, shared savings on next cycle |
| **Pilot economics** | One unit / one line · meter before/after · win-win: they get data, you get a case study |

Use your **financial coherence / scenario → $** habit: change coating coverage or line length → forecast energy $ — don’t invent a % on cold email (NAV-42 site numbers stay “to substantiate under NDA”).
