## Clinical intuition (why this exists)

At the bedside, a single processed number often **lags** the room. The Vigilant idea is:

1. Sense enough physiologic / neural signal on a **patch** form factor  
2. Score **coherence / decoherence** across systems (not one channel alone)  
3. Surface **pre-recognition** — drift that shows up **minutes before** a conventional index or naked-eye change forces a scramble  

Same coherence language as maritime / Field Lock: alignment holds → stable; alignment drops → instability is already starting.
