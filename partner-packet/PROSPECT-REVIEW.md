# Prospect Review  
### “Everything I’d want to know” — before you engage

Not a formal background check (those need consent, vendors, and usually come late).  
This is a **state check on a person or partner** — same family as determine-state / Outward Gate.

> When someone is identified as a prospect (hire, collaborator, investor, customer, intro), assemble the packet you’d actually want — then decide: engage, hold, or walk away.

**Name:** Prospect Review  
**Trigger:** labeled as prospect  
**Output:** a short dossier + a clear next move  
**Not:** silent legal background check on day one

---

## What you’d usually want to know

### 1. Who they are
- Full name + how they present publicly  
- Role / company / what they claim to do  
- Location / timezone if it matters  
- How they found you (or how you found them)

### 2. Public face
- Website, LinkedIn, GitHub, X  
- Does the story match across surfaces?  
- Any obvious red flags in tone or claims?

### 3. Work you can verify
- Live links, shipped products, papers, patents (public)  
- “Talks big / shows little” vs “shows work”  
- Overlap with your lanes (art, math, defense, clinical, apps)

### 4. Fit to *this* ask
- What do *they* want?  
- What do *you* want from them? (one ask)  
- Skills / capital / access / customers — which?

### 5. Risk & trust (lightweight)
- Conflicts of interest you can see  
- Prior disputes, lawsuits, or burned partners (public only)  
- Do they pressure for secrets / claim charts too fast?  
- NDA readiness if depth is needed

### 6. Communication
- How they write / show up on a call  
- Clarity vs fog  
- Respect for your Outward Gate (not asking for the vault on first touch)

### 7. Decision
| Call | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Engage** | Take the next step (call, one-pager, small task) |
| **Hold** | Need one more fact |
| **Vault only** | Interesting, but NDA before anything deep |
| **Pass** | Not a fit — no drama required |

---

## What this is *not*

- Not pulling credit / criminal records without a proper process  
- Not stalking private accounts  
- Not a substitute for later formal checks if you hire someone  
- Not dumping your IP because they seemed nice

Public looking + structured questions = Prospect Review.  
Formal background check = separate, later, consented.

## Where it sits in your stack

1. Relax before engage  
2. **Prospect Review** (if the “object” is a person)  
3. Determine state of the work/deal  
4. Fast loop  
5. Outward Gate before anything you send them  
6. Closure

People get a review. Outbound materials get a gate. Both before you’re in deep.
