## How to run the room (5–7 minutes)

1. **Problem** (20 sec) — “Here’s a real pain…”  
2. **Open demo** (2–3 min) — click, don’t lecture  
3. **What changed** (20 sec) — the “solved” sentence  
4. **Spine** (20 sec) — “Under it: signal, adaptive loop, spectral math. Math is out there; most people don’t read it.”  
5. **Second demo** (optional) — different lane (e.g. Lock → NAV-42)  
6. **Ask** (15 sec) — one ask  
7. **Stop**
