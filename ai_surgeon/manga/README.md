# 黒塔と湖 — Kuro-tō to Mizūmi

A **manga** gift for a daughter and a son.

Built from the Warrior Surgeon story (the original writer). Same storyline.
Sparse places filled. Japanese characters. Good conquers evil. Wholesome,
intelligent, moral leads who teach a lesson in everything they do.
The last thing left is a residue of love.

## What was made wholesome for the gift

- Romance becomes a miko’s vow and a student’s courage. No bedroom fanservice.
- Midsummer is white yukata, song, and the Holy Well — not a nude rite.
- The tent uses water and a song, not whiskey.
- “Become the warrior” is filled as **武は守り** — the warrior is the one who fights *for* life.
- Solomon’s line still stands: there is no Warrior. Just the Surgeon.
- The rebellion is not an army of killing. He starves cruelty by healing. Good conquers evil.
