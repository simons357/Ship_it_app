television file. **Not a finished TV file.** A parent reads the episode in the browser. Godot and Unreal later consume
the same beats (`engine/storyboard.json` + `manga/script.json`).

## The current (always)

**You are loved.**  
あなたは、愛されている。

Every room has to leave that sentence in the air, even when nobody says it. The world can
feel like it is falling apart. Children still need a heartwarming story about love and
peace. Put in everything you would want for your own children: rest, names, unlocked doors,
hands that look before they cut, a teacher who tells the truth, a lake that is a gift.

## Craft patterns only (do not paste books or lyrics)

## Ten small laws of the house (story events, not a lecture)

Worked in as things people **do**:

1. **No other gods.** The Tower wants to be the source. Jun asks to see its light. It cannot.
2. **No idols of power.** Copies of Kirana’s voice and of children who are almost Kaito.
   “You cannot duplicate a soul and call it love.”
3. **Do not cheapen the holy Name.** Kirana came at the will of God. Solomon: *We do not own
   your soul. We hold your promise.* They do not use sacred words as a sales pitch.
4. **Rest.** After the tent, Solomon makes him take the days. The beach is a Sabbath of salt
   and ordinary sun. Work, then rest.
5. **Honor father and mother.** The childhood house. The spare key is gone; he does not smash
   the door. “They love you, Jun. But they are not here.” He wants to see them before time
   eats the photograph.
6. **Do not kill.** Solomon: you are saving lives, not taking them. Choir, not army. 武は守り.
7. **Keep the vow clean.** Miko’s vow, student’s courage. Midsummer is white yukata, song,
   Holy Well — not a bedroom. No fanservice.
8. **Do not steal.** He puts the ceremonial mask back. He pays for the water. He tosses the
   denarii to Solomon instead of pocketing them. He will not steal the Tower’s blue tools to
   write the songs.
9. **Do not lie.** He does not pretend to speak a language he does not have — he uses tone.
   He uses Makoto’s name, not the halo “Prisoner.” He tells Solomon the truth about the lake.
10. **Do not covet.** The contract’s zeroes are not why he signs. He does not covet the title
    Warrior or King so he can feel tall. He wants the children to inherit kindness, not rank.

## Visual language

Japanese manga / cel: vermilion, gold, lake teal, paper, thick ink, speed lines in the sky
only. Respect, not costume. Children readable. No gore close-ups; the tent is honest but
not a horror show. No whiskey. No nude rite.
