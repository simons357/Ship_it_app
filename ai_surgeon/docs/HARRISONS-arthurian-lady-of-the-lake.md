## Voice

Lyrical, romantic, Arthurian. The Oracle (later Kyranna) is a Sleeping Queen / Lady of the Lake figure: electric outline, crescent on the forehead, double-helix serpents, “Nature always bends to the will of the Queen.” She calls Occam **Warrior, Healer, King**. She tells him to climb, then pulls him into visions and songs.

Quoted from the extract, not invented:

> “I am a priestess of Avalon. I came here at the will of God, to guide those chosen to their purpose.”

> “Welcome to Avalon.”

> “I will teach you to master the lake. Consciousness is liquid.”

> I followed her through the mist of Avalon

The songs at the end of the blob (“Lady of The Lake,” “Angel Black,” “Avalon,” “Midsummer baby,” “Young together”) belong here. They are not menu copy.

## What this voice wants

- “Become the warrior.”
- “Be brave, my Warrior, my Healer, my King.”
- Destiny, light-has-no-shadows, the Tower as temptation vs the Lake as truth.
- Time moving differently for the Queen.

Solomon, in the other split, answers the opposite: *“Never. There is no Warrior. Just the Surgeon.”*
