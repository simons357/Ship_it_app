## Voice

Literary military sci-fi. First Lieutenant John Occam, F-22, is taken into a Black Tower and told he has been inducted into an elite surgical unit under **Air Combat Command**. Solomon (training officer) is dry, contractual, and anti-mystic: *“Never. There is no Warrior. Just the Surgeon.”*

Roman cellar, the lanista: *“Nisi moritur, refice eum.”* *“Unless he’s dying, fix him.”*

The training metaphor is a tower of floors, not a civilian residency year-chart. Hub numbering (Module 12 appendectomy, Module 21 tube) does **not** come from this document.

## What the text actually trains

Early floors, in order of appearance:

| Floor / room | Case | What it is teaching |
|---|---|---|
| Orientation / 1023 | Hometown sim, contract, key | Consent, leave-when-you-want, you pay for instruments |
| 1029 jungle | Laceration, irrigation, ant-mandible suture | Field improvisation. Look at the wound. An assistant (the boy) fetches tools |
| Roman cellar | Soldier: closed reduction, wine wash, debridement, suture | Examine before you cut. Assistant as the second seat. Pay in denarii for the tray |
| Confederate tent | Traumatic amputation under fire | The clock does not pause. No introductions. Assistant holds the field. Hot-iron haemostasis |

Later floors are not written in this drop. The rest of the blob turns to the Oracle / Lady of the Lake (Harrison’s voice — [`HARRISONS-arthurian-lady-of-the-lake.md`](HARRISONS-arthurian-lady-of-the-lake.md)).

## Military / trauma framing (what may touch the hub)

These ideas already live on the civilian hub; this novel is why they are not optional colour:

- **Trauma runs a live clock.** The tent scene is “Doc, Doc, Doc” with no briefing. Module 21 already has live vitals and patient loss enabled.
- **Identify before you cut.** Occam palpates the fracture, pours wine, *then* cuts dead tissue. He does not get a transparent-anatomy upgrade until the Civil War case, and even then he looks first. Hub copy: ASK / MISS / HIT.
- **Seats.** Jungle boy, Roman assistant, tent orderly — someone else is holding, fetching, watching. Playable HTML is still single-seat with an AI attending; locked seats on the hub remain surgeon / anaesthesia / scrub. Do not invent a playable co-op from this novel.
- **Pilot ↔ surgeon.** Rapid decisions, protocol, team talk under fire. That is character motivation, not a second product.

## Constraints taken from this voice

1. Solomon’s line stands for the **shipped game title**: AI Surgeon, not Warrior Surgeon. The Oracle’s “Become the warrior” stays in Harrison’s file.
2. Blank-slate / no prior medical habits is a plot device. The desk’s clinical content is still Jonathan Simons, CRNA — do not replace the CRNA ladder with “they wanted a pilot.”
3. Instruments, help, and insurance cost money in the Tower. The hub has **no checkout**.
4. Not a medical device. Not a clinical reference. Field surgery in the novel is fiction.
5. Do not dump F-22 / pyramid / Tower / Occam onto playable chrome.
