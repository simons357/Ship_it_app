## Chemistry — not “H2O3”

| Claim you hear | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Drinkable water is **H₂O** | True |
| Stable bulk **H₃O₂** (“structured / hexagonal bottle”) | **Not accepted chemistry** — formula/stability arguments fail; clusters in liquid water are fleeting |
| **H₂O₂** (hydrogen peroxide) | Different chemical — not drinking “structured water” |
| Pollack **exclusion zone (EZ)** near some hydrophilic surfaces | Lab phenomenon (microspheres excluded); **mechanism disputed** (structure change vs diffusiophoresis / ion gradients) |
| EZ = stable bottle of superior drinking water | **Not established** |

Critical review (open access): [IJMS 2020 — EZ phenomena](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7404113/).  
Chemist overview: [The Conversation — structured water claims](https://theconversation.com/dont-fall-for-the-snake-oil-claims-of-structured-water-a-chemist-explains-why-its-nonsense-188159).

**Pollack himself** (quoted in consumer press): he has **not** been convinced that commercial “structured / fourth-phase” products are producing what his lab studies — he asks for evidence (e.g. spectral checks), not hand-waving.

| Claim type | Evidence level |
| --- | --- |
| Clean water hydrates; minerals/taste matter for preference | Normal, boring, true |
| Commercial vortex / “structured” water **clinically superior** in humans | **No solid consensus**; large rigorous human trials largely absent |

**Safe path for AquaQuarts:** luxury **experience** (vessel, story, Lithia/spring sourcing, tableside ritual).
