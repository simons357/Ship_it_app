The central target sits:
\[
(T_c)_+^2
\le
C_{\mathrm{geom}}\,\mathcal D_s\,E\,Y.
\]
Amplitude reduction, spectral identities, and viscosity
packaging agree. Four corrections:

1. \(|T_c(u)|\le C\|u\|_2 X^{3/2}\) is false as a
   universal estimate. Under \(u=av\), left side \(a^3\),
   right side \(a^4\). Discarded by algebra. Not an open
   gap toward ★. Do not apply this death to Attack-2
   \(C_*\) in \(|T_c|\le C_* X^{3/2}\Lambda\).

2. ★ \(\Rightarrow\) global regularity in this packaging
   is supported. “Equivalent to global regularity” is
   not. No converse is written.

3. \(T_c(-v)=-T_c(v)\) while \(\mathcal D_s,E,Y\) stay
   even. A universal bound on \((T_c)_+^2\) is the same
   as a universal bound on \(T_c^2\). A large negative
   value deserves attention: reverse the field.

4. Section 4 of the older proof attempt does not
   establish its advertised theorem. Archive it.
   Do not label that opening claim proved.

Near-shell: \(\mathcal R_\star\to K_{\alpha,\beta}(w)\)
only for aligned, sign-selected \(z_\beta\). Arbitrary
\(z_\beta\) sees the projection against \(B(w,w)\).
