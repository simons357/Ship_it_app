# Grok Imagine — AquaQuarts decanter prompts

Paste each block into Grok Imagine as a **single prompt**.  
No logos, no text on bottle, no plastic, no lab gear, no medical vibes.

**Shared physical brief (both lines):**  
Earthen stone body · inner quartz lining visible at the rim · purple glass accents · forever tableside decanter · fine dining · soft museum lighting.

---

## 1) Cherokee-country / Lithia line

```text
Product render of a luxury water decanter for fine dining, three-quarter view on a dark walnut table, soft candlelight and shallow depth of field.

Form: tall heirloom pitcher-decanter, about 28–32 cm high, hand-crafted earthen stone body in warm river-clay and soft ochre tones with subtle mica flecks, matte mineral texture like polished river stone. Interior lined with translucent milky quartz that catches light at the wide pouring rim. Accent panels and a slender neck ring in deep amethyst purple glass — translucent, not neon — fused into the stone like stained glass set in earth.

Aesthetic: Southeastern woodland / Cherokee-country craft partnership — organic curves, quiet dignity, no cartoon patterns, no headdresses, no sacred symbols, no written language glyphs, no feathers glued on. Think artisan vessel from Georgia mineral-spring country: grounded, ceremonial, hospitable.

Mood: intentional water ritual, anniversary table, guest will take this home forever. No plastic. No labels. No text. No barcode. Photoreal studio product photography, 85mm lens, natural materials only, elegant negative space.
```

**Negative / avoid (add if the tool supports it):** plastic bottle, Perrier, logo, English text, Native costume stereotype, dreamcatcher, neon purple, sci-fi, lab beaker, QR code, watermark.

---

## 2) Scandinavian / Nordic line

```text
Product render of a luxury water decanter for fine dining, three-quarter view on pale ash wood, cool north-window light, airy Scandinavian interior blur.

Form: tall minimal heirloom decanter, about 28–32 cm high, refined earthen stone body in cool greige and soft slate, ultra-smooth almost porcelain stone finish. Interior lined with clear-to-milky quartz visible as a thin luminous collar at the rim. Precise geometric purple glass inset — a single vertical amethyst glass blade or ring — clean, architectural, translucent, never glittery.

Aesthetic: Scandinavian flair — quiet luxury, honest materials, soft rounded rectangle silhouette, perfect proportions, Hygge restraint. No runes, no flags, no text, no Viking costume cues. Nordic spa meets Michelin tableside service.

Mood: intentional water from a northern spring, forever vessel the guest keeps. No plastic. No labels. Photoreal product photography, 85mm lens, soft shadows, museum clarity, elegant negative space.
```

**Negative / avoid:** plastic, IKEA logo, text, rune stickers, horned helmets, neon, lab, cluttered table.

## Quick use

1. Generate Cherokee-country prompt → pick best → iterate (“more quartz at rim”, “less purple”, “shorter neck”).  
2. Generate Scandinavian prompt the same way.  
3. Drop finals into `docs/aquaquarts/renders/` when you download them.  
4. Use in brochure / investor one-pager / maker brief.
