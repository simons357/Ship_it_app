## Executive summary

AquaQuarts is a **luxury tableside water ritual**: natural spring or place water served in an heirloom **earthen stone · quartz-look · purple glass** decanter. A **little book tied to the neck** opens a website — source film, blessing or poem, and a promise to **protect and celebrate water and the environment**. The guest **takes the decanter home** (date-night forever reminder). **No single-use plastic.**

Cities and countries can license **Place Editions** to show off their best water and national/local pride. Money flows to the well/source, makers, and a published stewardship %.

**Not in scope:** medical claims, structured-water pseudoscience, Millennium math, “almost zero-carbon zeppelins” until real.

**One line:** *Sell intentional place-water in forever vessels; pay the spring and the planet’s story; scale by letting the world put its best water in the ritual.*

## 4. Market & taste (honest)

- Blind tests: soft clean municipal water (e.g. NYC-style) often wins; **high-TDS mineral water may lose sips**.  
- Industry preference band often **~200–400 mg/L TDS**; Ca/Mg/HCO₃ help; high Na/Cl hurt.  
- AquaQuarts sells **ritual + object + meaning**, not “best blind taste.”  
- Detail: `WATER-TASTE-INDUSTRY-DATA.md`
