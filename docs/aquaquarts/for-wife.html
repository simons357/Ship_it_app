“It looks great — but maybe they’d say yeah that’s a great idea, but we don’t need Jon.”

An idea is not property. AquaQuartz can be. If the name, vessel designs, booklet, website, and Lithia / restaurant contracts live inside Jon’s LLC, and he keeps founder control, they cannot run the real business without him — unless he sells them the company. We trademark and contract early so “we don’t need Jon” is legally false.

What it is

Restaurants serve water from a named place (start: Lithia Springs) in a stone-and-glass decanter with a little book on the neck. Guest takes the decanter home. The book opens a website — spring story, blessing or poem, give-back to water causes. No plastic bottle. Later: Finland and other Place Editions.

Not medical “structured water.” Art + ritual + place + stewardship.

The water recipe

Does the quartz lining bring anything? Can we tweak pH to be world-class?

Quartz: yes for beauty, brand name, and story — Lithia water already formed through quartz-bearing granite. The vessel echoes the rock. It does not meaningfully change pH or “charge” the water. Don’t sell quartz-chemistry miracles.

Best sip in the world: possible for a separate House Sip water — moderate minerals (TDS ~150–400), calcium / magnesium / bicarbonate, low sodium, pH about 7.2–7.8, served cold. That is a soft spring or carefully remineralized purified water. Lithia stays untouched (~2,300 TDS, pH ~7.4) — rich heritage mineral character, not Evian. Two products; honest labels.
