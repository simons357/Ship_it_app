AquaQuartz

Intentional place-water in a forever vessel.

Named water from a real place. A stone-and-glass decanter the guest keeps. A little book on the neck that tells the story. No throwaway plastic.

Lithia Springs launch concept — warm clay body, quartz collar, amethyst ring, neck book.

What it is

  1. 01

    The decanter

    Earthen stone · quartz-look liner · purple or local glass. Designed as art. Built to leave with the guest.

  2. 02

    The water

    Lithia Springs first — honest mineral spring character. Cities next (Savannah hometown pride). Optional House Sip later for contest-level taste.

  3. 03

    The neck book

    Tied to the vessel. Opens a site: source film, blessing or poem, give-back, protect-and-celebrate water.

  4. 04

    Place pride

    Cities and countries license editions — their water, their poem, their craft cues. Guests take civic pride home.

We sell art + ritual + place pride + stewardship. Blind sip contests are optional for some editions — not Lithia’s job. No structured-water health claims. No fake tribal endorsements.

Order of operations

  1. Protect — LLC owns IP; trademark AquaQuartz; NDAs.
  2. Lithia deal — water + story rights in writing.
  3. Samples — 10–20 vessels + neck book + simple site.
  4. One restaurant — Savannah or Atlanta / Charleston pilot.
  5. Learn price / COGS — then 100–200 unit run + insurance.
  6. Savannah edition — moss-neck samples; city pride story.
  7. Place Editions — after Lithia (and ideally Savannah) work.

What we will not claim

No structured-water health miracles · no lithium dosing · no fake tribal endorsements · no charity % until contracted · no trademarked spring names without license · blind taste is not the product — ritual and object are.

AquaQuartz = named place water + forever quartz vessel + neck book + city pride + give-back. City editions: catalytic carbon + cold is usually enough. Lithia: leave the spring alone. The vessel is art — not a chemistry lab.
