The line

These are bottles — not gadgets.

AquaQuartz is a tall stone water bottle for the table: pour, serve, take home. Place Editions keep the same bottle language and change the stone, water, and story.

Place Editions

Same bottle. Different place.

Each city licenses its water, poem, and stone. The silhouette stays a bottle — tall enough to pour, proud enough to keep.
