## Her question, answered up front

> “It looks great — but maybe they’d say yeah that’s a great idea, but we don’t need Jon.”

**Short answer:** An *idea* is not property. **AquaQuartz** can be. If the **name, vessel designs, booklet, website, and Lithia / restaurant contracts** sit inside **Jon’s LLC**, and he keeps **founder control**, they cannot run the real business without him — unless he sells them the company. We trademark and contract early so “we don’t need Jon” is legally false.

You cannot trademark “nice water in a pretty bottle.” You **can** own **AquaQuartz**, the **look of the decanter**, the **ritual kit**, and the **deals**. That is the fence.

## 1. What it is (one breath)

Restaurants serve water from a **named place** (start: **Lithia Springs**) in a **beautiful stone-and-glass decanter** with a **little book tied to the neck**. Guest **takes the decanter home**. Book opens a website — spring story, blessing or poem, money to water causes. **No plastic bottle.** Later: Finland and other **Place Editions**.

We are **not** selling medical “structured water.” We sell **art + ritual + place + stewardship**.

## 3. How we protect it (do this early — small money)

*Not legal advice — use a Georgia trademark / IP attorney. Order matters.*

| Step | What | Why | Rough cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Confirm **LLC** owns everything (assignment from Jon) | Company holds IP | Already have LLC, or ~$200–$500 + agreement update |
| 2 | **Trademark search + file** “AquaQuartz” (+ logo) at USPTO | Stops others using the name on water / hospitality goods | ~$250–$750 filing + ~$1–2k attorney |
| 3 | Copyright: keep dated design files, booklet, site (optional registration) | Proof you made it | $0–hundreds |
| 4 | Consider **design patent** on final lead decanter | Blocks copycat vessel look | Often $2–5k+ |
| 5 | **Maker / spring / restaurant contracts:** IP stays with LLC; NDA | They can’t walk with Jon’s drawings | Templates ~$1–3k |
| 6 | **Investor terms:** Jon stays creative lead / managing member; don’t sell 100% | Money doesn’t erase the founder | Counsel on any raise |

**Moat:** Trademark + designs + Lithia relationship (exclusivity if you can get it) + first restaurants + Jon’s art. Idea alone = weak. **Owned brand + object + source = stronger.**

## 4. Order of operations (what we do first → later)

### Phase 0 — Protect & decide · ~$1–4k
1. Lock name **AquaQuartz**, start **Lithia Springs**.  
2. LLC owns IP; Jon = founder.  
3. Trademark search / file + pick one logo.  
4. No full maker packs to strangers without NDA.

### Phase 1 — Lithia + samples · ~$10–25k
5. Talk to **Lithia Spring Water / well** — buy or license water + story rights (written).  
6. Quote **2–3 makers** (Georgia ceramic + glass accent; maybe one OEM).  
7. Make **10–20 sample decanters** + neck book mockup + simple webpage.  
8. Photograph real samples (better than AI for investors).

### Phase 2 — First restaurant pilot · ~$15–40k more
9. Approach **1–2** fine-dining rooms (Atlanta / Savannah / Charleston).  
10. Place pilot kits (decanter + water + neck book).  
11. Learn real guest price; fix COGS.  
12. Pay Lithia + makers every unit; give-back only if a **written %** after the math works.

### Phase 3 — Scale or raise · ask below
13. If pilots work: **100–200** units, insurance, proper labels.  
14. Optional Nordic edition.  
15. Place Editions **after** Lithia works.

**Do not** start with 50 countries. **Lithia first.**

## 6. How much you can make (honest)

**One night example:** Guest pays **~$280** (water + keeps decanter). Restaurant keeps ~**45%** (~$126). Brand gets ~**$154**. Making the kit ~**$70–$150** (goal ~**$110**). Tens of dollars left per kit before give-back and overhead.

**Refills** (guest already owns decanter): better margin.

| Scale | Kits / year | Ballpark brand revenue | Character |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pilot | 50–80 | ~$8k–$15k | Learning; often a **loss** year |
| Early | 200–400 | ~$30k–$60k | Still thin after costs |
| Working brand | 750–1,500 | ~$100k–$250k+ | Possible living product line |
| Place licenses | Extra | Fees / royalties | Upside if cities care |

**Not a promise of riches.** Path: art object + hospitality + place pride. Year 1 is usually **proof**, not profit.
