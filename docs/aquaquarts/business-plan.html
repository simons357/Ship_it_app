AquaQuarts

Intentional water. Forever vessel. Place pride. No plastic.

Natural place-water in an heirloom stone and purple-glass decanter. A little book tied to the neck carries the source, a blessing or poem, and a promise to protect and celebrate the environment. The guest takes it home. Cities and countries can show off their best water.

1. Executive summary

AquaQuarts is a luxury tableside ritual — not a science claim, not a plastic bottle brand. Revenue from restaurant kits, refills, DTC heirlooms, and Place Edition licenses. Pilot capital roughly $40k–$120k; year one is proof, not profit.

One line: Sell intentional place-water in forever vessels; pay the spring and the stewardship story; scale by letting the world put its best water in the ritual.
