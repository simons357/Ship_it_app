Why this exists

Fancy water is still plastic. Meaning is missing.

Premium bottled water fights on blind sips it often loses to good tap. Single-use plastic still reaches the ocean. Fine dining has wine ritual — and almost nothing equally memorable for water.

AquaQuarts sells the night: a forever object, a place’s best water, stewardship you can point to, and a date that stays on the shelf.

What we do

The ritual kit

  1. 01

    The decanter

    Earthen stone · quartz-look liner · purple glass. Designed as art. Built to leave with the guest.

  2. 02

    The water

    Lithia Springs first — natural mineral spring as-is (character, not Evian). Quartz liner = art & geology story, not a pH machine. Optional later: House Sip engineered for preference (TDS 150–400, pH ~7.2–7.8). See WATER-RECIPE.md.

  3. 03

    The neck book

    Tied to the vessel. Opens a site: source film, blessing or poem, give-back %, protect-and-celebrate water.

  4. 04

    The memory

    At home the book and decanter keep reminding them of the date — and that the spend meant something.

  5. 05

    Place pride

    Cities and countries license editions — their water, their poem, their craft cues — 50-concept palette ready.

Water is life made visible… we protect springs, rivers, and oceans, celebrate the source, and refuse the throwaway bottle.
