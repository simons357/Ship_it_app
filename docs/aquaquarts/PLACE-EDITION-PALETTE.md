# AquaQuarts — 5×10 place-edition palette

**Idea:** Each city/country shows off **their** water + pride in a forever stone/glass decanter.  
**Always on the vessel:** little **book tied to the neck** → website with source film, blessing or poem, and a short reminder that water and environment are precious — protect and celebrate them.  
**No plastic.** Guest takes the decanter home.

**Makers & money:** see `MANUFACTURERS-AND-FINANCIALS.md` (summary § below).

---

## Shared ritual kit (every edition)

| Element | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| Vessel | Earthen stone / mineral body · quartz-look liner · purple glass accent (local color swap OK) |
| Neck book | Leather/cord-tied booklet on the neck |
| Inside the book | Origin story · map/photo · blessing **or** poem (local language + English) · “Protect & celebrate our water” · give-back % · QR to film |
| Pride layer | Subtle place cues in **form/finish/glaze** — not costume stereotypes; partners approve art |
| Water | **Local-first** spring/utility “best water” under contract |

## Neck book — fixed short environmental text (all editions)

Print in every booklet (local language + English):

> Water is life made visible. This vessel holds a place’s best water — and a promise: we protect springs, rivers, and oceans, celebrate the source, and refuse the throwaway bottle. May you remember this night — and the living world that made it possible.

Blessing **or** poem follows on the next page (partner-written).

## How to pitch a country / city

> Your best water. Your poem or blessing. Your pride in the vessel. Guests keep the decanter; the neck book sends them to your source film and a promise to protect water. We design and place it in fine dining; you supply water and culture partners; a published share funds watershed and global water projects.
