Origins

Lithia Springs. Finland. Your town’s water.

The core story is Lithia Springs, Georgia — mineral-spring heritage and Cherokee-country craft explored with fair economics. A second artist line can source Finnish water with Scandinavian vessel flair. A third dimension: place editions with cities and towns — their water, their story, most of the margin to named water projects that protect springs, rivers, and access worldwide.

Editions are hospitality and stewardship — not a claim that we manufacture water from air, and not a charity promise until the split is written down.
