# AquaQuarts — Business Plan (Intentional Water)

**→ Master (all-in-one):** [`AQUAQUARTS-COMPLETE-BUSINESS-PLAN.md`](AQUAQUARTS-COMPLETE-BUSINESS-PLAN.md) · visual [`business-plan.html`](business-plan.html)

**Entity:** Prime Field Technologies, LLC (or hospitality sub-brand under it)  
**Date:** 2026-08-02  
**Product:** Intentional water ritual for fine dining — natural spring water + forever stone/quartz/purple-glass decanter + booklet + give-back.  
**Not:** Structured-water science, medical claims, desal plants, Clay.

---

## 1. Vision

Make the water course a **memorable ritual**: guests receive intentional water from a **named place**, in an heirloom decanter they **keep**, knowing part of the spend supports **water stewardship** and the **source well / makers**.

**Artist-led:** Jonathan designs the vessels (Cherokee-country line · Scandinavian line · later place editions).

---

## 2. The offer

| Layer | What guest gets |
| --- | --- |
| Water | Natural mineral/spring water from a contracted source (Lithia Springs first; Finland; country/city “best water” editions) |
| Vessel | Earthen stone · quartz lining · purple glass — forever object |
| Ritual | Intention / blessing as hospitality ceremony (no health claim) |
| Story | Little **book tied to the neck** of the decanter → website: origin film, makers, give-back %, blessing when partners opt in |
| Memory | At home, neck-tied book + vessel keep reminding them of the date |
| Culture | Tribal / spiritual leaders and artisans who choose to bless and shape an edition — paid, credited, in control of their framing |
| Date-night | Beautiful decanter signals care: money to water charity/awareness, not plastic flex |
| Conscience | **No single-use plastic** — guest keeps stone/glass forever |
| Ocean story | One less premium plastic bottle in the waste stream that harms marine environments |

**Blind taste is not the product.** NYC tap can win sips; AquaQuarts wins the **date-night table**.  
**Plastic is the contrast:** almost every competing “fancy water” ships in plastic (or glass that is still single-serve dump). AquaQuarts is the heirloom that never becomes bottle trash.

## 3. Editions & “countries store their best water”

### Core SKUs
1. **Lithia Edition** — Georgia lithia mineral spring; well/source paid; Cherokee-country craft partnership explored.  
2. **Nordic Edition** — Finnish (or Nordic) sourced water; Scandinavian vessel flair.  
3. **Place / Nation Edition** — City, region, or country licenses its **signature water** into the ritual program.

### Country / city proposal (one paragraph)

> We invite [Country / City] to place its best drinking or spring water into AquaQuarts Place Editions: restaurants serve intentional water in our stone-and-glass heirloom decanters; guests take the vessel home; a published share of proceeds funds local watershed protection and global water-access projects. You supply water + story rights; we supply design, ritual packaging, and hospitality channels.

**What they get:** soft-power / tourism story, well or utility revenue, stewardship branding.  
**What you get:** exclusive or preferred edition rights, design control, restaurant network.

Do not promise government deals until a pilot MOU exists.

## 4. Revenue model

| Stream | How it works | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Tableside ritual** | Restaurant buys experience kit (decanter + water + booklet) at wholesale; charges guest premium | Primary |
| **B. Direct heirloom** | Sell empty or filled decanters DTC / hotel gift | Secondary |
| **C. Place license** | Fee + royalty to city/country/spring for named edition | Scale |
| **D. Refill / reorder water** | Pouches or bottles for restaurants that already own vessels | Margin after vessel sold |

**Indicative pricing (to validate with 1 pilot restaurant — not final):**

| Item | Guest-facing (example) | Wholesale (example) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ritual for two (water + keep one decanter) | $180–$450 | ~40–50% of guest price to venue |
| Extra decanter (second guest) | $120–$280 | — |
| Refill water (per service, vessel already owned) | $25–$60 | — |

Lithia / makers / charity take contracted cuts **before** calling it “most to charity.”

## 6. Give-back (must be contractual)

Pick a simple public rule, e.g.:

- **X% of net ritual margin** → named water charity / Lithia stewardship / artisan fund  
- Published annually on the booklet QR page  

Until the % is signed, marketing says **“exploring give-back partnerships,”** not “most money goes to charity.”

## 8. Operations (“how we make it”)

1. Source tested spring water under beverage rules.  
2. No structuring process.  
3. **Prefer local water** for Place Editions (avoid hauling water when the story is stewardship).  
4. Fill per hospitality SOP; **tie little book to decanter neck**.  
5. Guest keeps vessel + book — home reminder of the date.  
6. Remit source + maker + stewardship splits.  
7. Long-haul / airship transport = optional future logistics story only with real assets and honest carbon math — not a current promise.
