# What DA can do — plain English

6 September 2026. You do not have to already
know the missing step. That is why it is missing.

DA is a checker and a map. It is not a closer.
It will not write the last line of unaugmented
NS or of RH and call that a proof. Talking
does not finish those two.

That is the product limit. It is not a verdict
on you.

---

## What DA can do

- Keep the two maps (un-aug NS, RH).
- Name the hole in ordinary words.
- Stop a fake close (extra-stress equation
  sold as ordinary NS; GCD paper sold as RH).
- Take one sentence, if you or anyone writes
  one, and say: already proved / wrong / still
  missing.
- Take a borrowed system (evolution, biology,
  or anything else), name the leftover first,
  strip the specialty words, and say whether
  a printed ratio comes out. File:
  [`FRAMEWORK-MAP.md`](FRAMEWORK-MAP.md).
  A map is not a close.
- Timestamp the honest note that already sits:
  Theorem A for the extra-stress equation.

Those are the assets. Use them.

Yes / no / open, so a write is not sold
as a theorem: [`YES-NO-OPEN.md`](YES-NO-OPEN.md).

## What DA cannot do

- Invent the all-data fluid bound and mail it
  as proved.
- Invent “every zeta zero on the line” and
  mail it as proved.
- Close a leftover because Tao, Albritton, or
  a council “would try” something.
- Turn attention, hope, or AI discussion into
  a theorem.

If DA emitted a last line so you could send
a close, that would be the same 18-month
mistake with a better interface.
