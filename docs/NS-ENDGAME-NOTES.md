## What the incoming note got right

The load-bearing hole is not another falsifier.

1. **Uniform triadic bound is not a theorem.**
   A growing list of killed configurations is not an argument
   that rules out every triadic configuration at once.
2. **The \(m^{1/2}\) heuristic has no lattice realization.**
   It is not confirmed dead and it is not a seated obstruction.
   Unresolved is the dangerous state.
3. **H1 on the cylinder is a separate track.**
   It is not folded into the main line. Decide dependency or
   parallel. Do not let it drift as an orphan, and do not start
   it from an SND page.

A case list is necessary and not sufficient.
More case-work does not exhaust the space.
What would move the hole is one of:

- a **covering**: the printed configurations cover the triadic
  space up to a controlled remainder, and that remainder is
  bounded directly;
- a **monotonicity / scaling** argument on \(\mathcal R_\star\)
  (or its analogue) so the worst case is a boundary already
  tested;
- a **spectral-gap argument on the triad operator itself**,
  named as that operator, not imported from another book.

The honest answer to “why is the case list exhaustive?” is
still: it is the list of things tried and killed.

## The three open objects

### 1. Uniform triadic bound

Not a theorem. Supporting cast until an exhaustion argument
sits. Isolated triads, wide/narrow AP, fixed-gap spheres,
designed \(\Theta(m^2)\), and the HH→L fan do not become a
covering by accumulation.

### 2. The \(m^{1/2}\) heuristic

Needs \(\Theta(m^2)\) aligned closures to threaten
\(T_c\sim m^{1/2}\). Continuum incidence (Hyp-ST★) is a
conditional density kill in a model, not a lattice transfer.
Hyp-Lat★ is missing. Until a short dismissal or a lattice
realization is written, leave it unresolved.

### 3. H1 on the cylinder

Leftover 1. A write is not this object. Cousins (P1, P1-loc,
PC) are not H1. Do not glue H1 to \(\star\). Do not start H1
from SND. Say whether it is a dependency of the uniform bound
or a parallel leftover — then keep that label.

triadic bound is not a bound. SND sitting is not a bound on
\(X\). Frequency drift from the field is still missing.
Displayed Theorem H stays withdrawn. \(A.2\) sits. \(A.3\) is
a ceiling, not a floor. Propagation unwritten.
