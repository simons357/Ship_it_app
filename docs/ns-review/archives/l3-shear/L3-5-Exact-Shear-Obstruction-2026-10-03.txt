L3-5: EXACT SHEAR OBSTRUCTION TO AN ENERGY-ONLY BUDGET
First-principles attack, 3 October 2026

RESULT

An explicit family of smooth, unforced, mean-zero, divergence-free solutions
of the full three-dimensional periodic Navier–Stokes equations has fixed
initial energy and viscosity, zero nonlinearity, and arbitrarily large
Lambda-weighted high-frequency L3 excess on every fixed positive time horizon.
Each solution is also an exact full Galerkin solution whenever the cutoff
contains its support. This is an integrated dynamical counterfamily, not just
an instantaneous admissible state.

It disproves a universal bound F(E0,nu,K,T) for the L3 budget, including a
choice K depending only on E0 and nu. It DOES NOT disprove L3-5 as written,
which fixes one smooth datum and allows K and the budget bound to depend on
that datum. It also does not refute the repeated-radius estimate, equation
(17), or global regularity. The original signed scalene budget is identically
zero on these examples.

2. EXACT FAMILY

Fix a>0 and nu>0. Let m,L be integers with 1<=L<=m. Define

g_{m,L}(y,t)=(a/sqrt(L)) sum_{r=m}^{m+L-1} exp(-nu r^2 t) exp(i r y),
u_{m,L}(x,y,z,t)=(Re g_{m,L}(y,t), 0, Im g_{m,L}(y,t)).

This is a real trigonometric-polynomial velocity. It has zero spatial mean
and divergence zero. Crucially,

(u dot grad)u=(Re g) partial_x u + (Im g) partial_z u=0.

Each component obeys partial_t u=nu partial_yy u. With constant pressure,
this proves the full unforced NSE exactly for every t>=0. There are no
generated modes. For every N>=m+L-1 it solves the full Galerkin ODE as well.

The Fourier coefficients are, for r=m,...,m+L-1,
u_(0,r,0)=(a/(2sqrt(L))) exp(-nu r^2t) (1,0,-i),
u_(0,-r,0)=conjugate(u_(0,r,0)).
For any two support modes p,q, q dot u_p=0. Thus every ordered interaction
vanishes before any summation or cancellation. All transfer blocks,
including repeated-radius and scalene blocks, vanish individually.

3. EXACT MOMENTS AND DISSIPATION

Since |u|=|g|, Parseval gives

E(t)=(a^2/L) sum_r exp(-2nu r^2t),
X(t)=(a^2/L) sum_r r^2 exp(-2nu r^2t),
Y(t)=(a^2/L) sum_r r^4 exp(-2nu r^2t).

In particular,
E(0)=a^2,
E'(t)=-2nu X(t),
X'(t)=-2nu Y(t),
integral_0^T X(t)dt=(a^2-E(T))/(2nu)<=a^2/(2nu),
m^2<=Lambda(t)<4m^2.

Initial energy and the total available energy-dissipation budget do not
increase with m or L. The spectral clock Lambda does increase with m.
Initial X and higher smoothness norms are NOT fixed across the family.

4. ANALYTIC L3 LOWER BOUND THROUGH A VISCOUS TIME INTERVAL

Put tau_m=1/(16nu m^2). For 0<=t<=tau_m and all support r<2m,
exp(-nu r^2t)>=exp(-1/4).

For |y|<=1/(2L), factor out the carrier exp(i m y):

Re(exp(-i m y)g(y,t))
  =(a/sqrt(L)) sum_{j=0}^{L-1} exp(-nu(m+j)^2t) cos(j y)
  >= (a/2) exp(-1/4) sqrt(L).

The inequality uses |j y|<1/2 and cos(j y)>=1/2. Carrier rotation does not
alter |g|, which is why two real velocity components are used.
The interval in y has normalized measure 1/(2pi L). Integration yields

||u(t)||_3 >= d a L^(1/6),
d=exp(-1/4)/(2(2pi)^(1/3))>0,
0<=t<=tau_m.

This is an analytic lower bound, not a numerical scaling fit.

5. INTEGRATED COUNTERFAMILY

For any fixed finite K, take m>K. Then h_{K,N}=u_{m,L} for all
N>=m+L-1. Whenever T>=tau_m, the preceding bounds give

S3_{K,N}(T)
 >= integral_0^{tau_m} [d a L^(1/6)-c nu]_+ m^2 dt
 = (1/(16nu)) [d a L^(1/6)-c nu]_+.

Fix a,nu,K,T>0. Choose L=m and let m tend to infinity. For sufficiently
large m, all hypotheses hold and the right side tends to infinity.
Consequently there is no finite universal F(a^2,nu,K,T) bounding S3 for
all smooth data of energy a^2 and all Galerkin cutoffs.

The time interval shrinks like m^(-2), but Lambda grows like m^2, so those
factors cancel exactly in the lower bound. L3 concentration survives that
cancellation. The same conclusion holds if the integral is restricted to
[tau_m/2,tau_m], with 1/32 in place of 1/16: no isolated-time pathology is
being exploited.

For these very same trajectories,
T_sc(h)=T_rep(h)=T(u)=0 and S_{K,N}(T)=0.

Therefore no uniform reverse comparison
S3_{K,N}(T)<=C S_{K,N}(T)+F(E0,nu,K,T)
can hold either. The seated direction S<=C_S S3+controlled is unaffected.

6. STRESS TESTS

Growing layer / frequency-localized case:
  L=m. All support lies in m<=|k|<2m. Energy is a^2, and the lower bound
  grows as a m^(1/6)/(16nu), up to fixed constants and the threshold.

Relative near-shell case:
  Let m=j^2 and L=j, with j tending to infinity. Relative radius width
  (L-1)/m tends to zero. Relative squared-radius width also tends to zero:
  ((m+L-1)^2-m^2)/m^2=O(1/j).
  Nevertheless S3 grows at least as a j^(1/6)/(16nu), up to constants.
  This is NOT a fixed absolute squared-radius strip; its absolute width
  grows. No claim against a fixed absolute-strip estimate follows.

Relative radial variance:
  If D_s=Z-Y^2/X with Z=||A^(3/2)u||_2^2, the enstrophy-weighted
  distribution of squared radii has support in [m^2,(m+L-1)^2]. Hence
  0<=D_s/(X Lambda^2)
    <=[((m+L-1)^2-m^2)/m^2]^2.
  This dimensionless variance tends to zero in the relative near-shell
  case, even while S3 diverges. This statement does not bound absolute D_s.

Single exact shell / pure carrier test:
  L=1 gives |u(t)|=a exp(-nu m^2t), Lambda=m^2 and E0=a^2.
  Set b=c nu. If a<=b, S3(T)=0. If a>b, set
  s=min(nu m^2T, log(a/b)). Direct integration gives
  S3(T)=[a(1-exp(-s))-b s]/nu.
  Thus S3(infinity)=[a-b-b log(a/b)]/nu, independent of m.
  High frequency alone does not cause divergence; growing occupancy does.

Amplitude test:
  The preceding exact formula grows with a/nu when a/nu grows. That does
  not by itself refute an energy-dependent bound, since E0=a^2 also grows.
  The main counterfamily fixes a and instead grows L.

Repeated-radius geometry:
  Every ordered nonlinear factor is zero throughout every trajectory.
  The repeated-radius bound holds trivially with zero on the left.
  Its validity cannot supply the missing universal energy-only S3 estimate.
  These collinear shear supports are admissible in the unrestricted claim;
  an estimate restricted to nondegenerate active transfer is a different
  statement and is not refuted here.

7. QUANTIFIER BOUNDARY -- WHAT IS AND IS NOT KILLED

FALSIFIED:
  For fixed E0,nu,K,T, a bound on S3 uniform over all smooth data of that
  energy and all N. Also falsified if finite K is selected only from E0,nu
  (or E0,nu,T): choose m larger than that K.

NOT FALSIFIED:
  For each fixed smooth u0 and nu, there exists K(u0,nu) such that for every
  finite T, sup_N S3_{K,N}(T)<infinity.

The family changes u0 as m,L change. For each one of its data, taking
K>=m+L-1 annihilates the high tail forever, so S3=0. Even with a smaller
fixed K, the solution is explicit and smooth and its individual budget is
finite. For cutoffs below the full support, the projected data are still
shears and have the same heat evolution.

No single smooth datum with diverging Galerkin budgets has been constructed.
No uniform initial-H1 counterfamily has been constructed. A bound depending
on initial X, higher datum norms, initial spectral tails, or other detailed
datum information is not excluded by this result.

Thus the precise conclusion is: the energy-only strengthening of L3-5 is
false. A datum-sensitive proof of L3-5 remains open. It would be an overclaim
to say all possible energy/geometry arguments have been ruled out.

8. RELATION TO THE CLAIMED EQUIVALENCE

L3-4 supplies a one-way quantitative comparison. It is not a two-way
comparison between the budgets, as the shear family demonstrates exactly.
The original criterion (17), combined with its conditional continuation
argument, may imply L3-5 through a uniform X bound and standard estimates.
That is a qualitative implication through the regularity conclusion, not
an algebraic interchangeability of the two integrands or energy-only bounds.
Nothing in this counterfamily contradicts such criterion-level equivalence.
