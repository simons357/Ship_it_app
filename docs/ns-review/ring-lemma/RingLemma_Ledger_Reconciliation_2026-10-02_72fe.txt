APRIL SOURCE: VERSION-SPECIFIC MATHEMATICAL FINDINGS

1. Geometric threshold.
The source uses E_c={|omega|>=c 2^j ||u||_2}. On an annulus, for divergence-free u, ||omega||_2 is comparable to 2^j||u||_2. Its asserted Bernstein inequality ||grad omega||_infinity<=C 2^(2j)||u||_2 omits the three-dimensional L2-to-L-infinity factor 2^(3j/2). The valid general estimate has exponent 7j/2 before division, hence 5j/2 for grad xi on E_c.

The corrected note's explicit family also refutes this version for sufficiently small fixed c. With n=2^(j-1), ||u_n||_2<=||omega_n||_2/n<=1/n, so c 2^j||u_n||_2<=2c. At the origin |omega_n|=1, hence the origin lies strictly in this original E_c for c<1/2, while the direction gradient grows as n^(5/2).

2. Q3 pairing.
For the displayed sign, <Q3,-Delta u> equals
alpha3 sum_l integral [|grad u|^2 |partial_l grad u|^2 + 2(grad u:partial_l grad u)^2].
It is not alpha3 integral |grad u|^4. With this positive quantity on the equation's right-hand side, it is growth, not dissipation. Correcting the sign requires a new PDE analysis.

3. Summation differs from the June source.
The April lemma explicitly ASSUMES an E_j^2 error bound; the June proof uses E_j^(3/2). The June missing E_j^(-1/2) factor must not be attributed verbatim to April. In April the band absorption calculation is valid at sufficiently high j*, under its assumed shell bounds. The proof leaves low-band cases and the off-band contribution uncontrolled and never derives those shell hypotheses from the displayed PDE. SND3/4 are not smallness estimates as written.

4. Main regularity proof.
Concentrated shell mass is not exact single-shell support. A finite time measure of spread events is not a bound on growth during those events. A finite-T estimate exp(C_M T) is not a uniform-in-time H1 bound. The claimed effective viscosity has the wrong sign for the displayed Q1. Smooth bootstrapping also needs the regularity of the nonlinear coefficients treated explicitly.

5. No zeta-clock section in this version.
The April file does not contain the June manuscript's zeta-clock and spacetime sections. Their errors belong to the June audit and must not be reported as content of this April file.
