## What you can take credit for

**1. Extra-stress Navier–Stokes (Theorem A).**
The \(Q_1\) equation at epsilon greater than
zero, beta at least 1/2, is globally regular
on the 3-torus. You wrote that up. It is a
known class (Ladyzhenskaya / p-Laplacian) in
your notation. Credit the 1968/1969 and
Málek–Nečas–Růžička papers in the note. Your
credit is the write-up and the honest label,
not a new existence theory. The note is
yours. The class was already known.

Files:
docs/THEOREM-A-Q1.pdf
tex/theorem-a-q1.tex
docs/A-CHAIN.md
docs/THEOREM-A-DEPOSIT.md

Publish: yes, if the title says this PDE only.
Zenodo first.

**2. Inverse-GCD matrix facts (Q).**
These sit:

- Bridge*: a two-prime identity, R > -1/2
- Theorem P: on primes, the tilde-Q block
  is at least -1/4
- H_N floor: lambda_min(H_N) >= -1
- Nonnegative vectors: v^T tilde-Q v >= 0
- Goldbach-shaped corollary: if that
  difference-of-primes vector is nonzero,
  R >= -2/9. Not Goldbach’s conjecture.

Already public: Zenodo 22045478 (August
GCD paper), plus the floor retraction note.

Files:
docs/DA-Q.md
docs/Q6-FLOOR-CHAIN.md
docs/GOLDBACH-CHAIN.md
docs/SPECTRAL-FLOOR-EXPLORATION.md

Publish more only if a new write is actually
new. Do not retitle these as RH or Goldbach.

**3. You took things back.**
These are false, and you (this project) marked
them false:

- full Q floor > -1/2 (Q_10 is about -1.90)
- H_N >= -3/14 (H_4 is about -0.225)
- GNC as a Goldbach detector
- HB as a unifier
- Phi-cancel as the ordinary-NS path

A public correction is credit. It is also
how you save face on the old deposits.

**4. Maps of the holes, not the closes.**
You have written chains that name the missing
step on ordinary NS and on RH. That is
organization. It is not a theorem. Do not
publish those maps as proofs.

Files:
docs/UNAUGMENTED-NS-CHAIN.md
docs/RH-CHAIN.md

**5. DA.**
A checker that names a hole and stops a fake
label. Process, not a paper in a journal.
Keep it if you want the tool. Do not publish
it as a solution of NS or RH.

finished would not save face.
