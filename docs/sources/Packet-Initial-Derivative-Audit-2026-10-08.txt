INITIAL DERIVATIVE PACKET: EXACT AUDIT AND THRESHOLD DISTINCTION
Date: 2026-10-08

Verdict

The supplied packet's production, Euler production derivative, viscous
production derivative, and parallel/orthogonal production split agree with
an independent exact Gaussian-polynomial calculation on R^3. This audit
also identifies the zero viscous strain term analytically and distinguishes
production growth from enstrophy growth. It supplies no finite-time estimate.

The uploaded source is preserved separately as original_packet.md. Its
finite-grid convergence claim and spectral-centroid values are not checked
here: domain, centroid definition, and the original numerical runner were
not supplied. The exact calculations below concern the stated Gaussian on
R^3, integrated with unnormalized Lebesgue measure. A periodic computation
must specify its periodization and box size before being identified with it.

1. Definitions and exact profile integrals

A(x,y,z) = exp(-|x|^2/2) (xy, xz, x+yz), U = curl A,
omega = curl U, S(v) = (grad v + grad v^T)/2.

E0 = integral |U|^2                 = (5/2) pi^(3/2)
X0 = integral |omega|^2             = (41/4) pi^(3/2)
Y0 = integral |Delta U|^2           = (423/8) pi^(3/2)
T0 = integral omega . S(U) omega    = (4 sqrt(6)/27) pi^(3/2)
                                   = 2.020675898176543.

Here X is the full squared L2 vorticity norm, without a factor 1/2.
For smooth divergence-free fields, Y = integral |grad omega|^2.

2. The zero viscous strain term is a general identity

For any smooth divergence-free u on R^3 with sufficient decay, or on a
periodic domain, write w = curl u. Then

I = integral w . S(Delta u) w
  = integral w . (w . grad) Delta u
  = -integral Delta u . (w . grad) w
  = integral curl w . (w . grad) w
  = integral curl w . grad(|w|^2/2)
  = 0.

The third line uses div w = 0. The fourth uses Delta u = -curl w.
The fifth uses (w . grad)w = grad(|w|^2/2) - w cross curl w.
The last uses div(curl w) = 0 and vanishing boundary terms.

Thus the numerical zero in the source is explained analytically. It is
not a special cancellation unique to this Gaussian packet. This assertion
does not automatically apply to bounded domains with nonzero boundary terms.

For the stated packet, exact Gaussian integration additionally gives

DT(U)[Delta U] = 2 integral Delta omega . S(U) omega
              = -16 T0
              = -32.33081437082469.

The factor -16 is profile-specific; the zero strain term is general.

3. Exact Euler derivative, including the pressure contribution

Let f = -(U . grad)U and v = P f = f - grad p, Delta p = div f.
Let w_E = curl v = (omega . grad)U - (U . grad)omega.
Then

DT(U)[v] = 2 integral w_E . S(U) omega
           + integral omega . S(v) omega.

All constants in the following list multiply (pi/2)^(3/2):

Vorticity contribution:              77/8
Unprojected strain contribution:    -519/128
Pressure strain contribution:      -371539/274560
Total strain contribution:         -742397/137280
Total Euler derivative:             578923/137280

Consequently

C_E = DT(U)[-P((U . grad)U)]
    = (578923/137280) (pi/2)^(3/2)
    = 8.302203014466292.

The full strain contribution is -10.646546451480994. These exact values
agree with the rounded +8.302 and -10.647 in the uploaded packet.

How the nonlocal term was evaluated:

Set g = div f and h = sum_ij partial_i partial_j(omega_i omega_j).
The pressure contribution is -integral h Delta^(-1)g. Both h and g are
polynomials times exp(-|x|^2). With the Fourier transform
F(k) = integral exp(-i k.x) f(x) dx, Parseval writes this as

(2 pi)^(-3) integral conjugate(h_hat(k)) g_hat(k) / |k|^2 dk.

Fourier transforms of polynomial Gaussians follow from
FT[x^alpha exp(-|x|^2)] = i^|alpha| partial_k^alpha
                         [pi^(3/2) exp(-|k|^2/4)].

Their product has Gaussian weight exp(-|k|^2/2). For an even monomial
k^alpha of total degree d, its normalized Gaussian integral divided by
|k|^2 is its ordinary normalized Gaussian moment divided by d+1. This
follows by separating radial and angular integration in three dimensions.
Odd moments vanish. The checker implements these operations with exact
rational arithmetic; floating point is used only to display final values.
A Parseval normalization equality without |k|^(-2) is checked separately.

4. Orthogonality labels

integral omega . w_E = T0,
||w_E||_2^2 = (87/8) (pi/2)^(3/2).

With alpha = T0/X0 and w_perp = w_E - alpha omega:

alpha = 0.03540364578548405
||w_perp||_2^2 / ||w_E||_2^2 = 0.9966585453814284
||w_perp||_2   / ||w_E||_2   = 0.9983278746891867.

The source's 0.9967 is correct as a squared-norm fraction. Its label
"orthogonal fraction" should specify this convention.

The parallel contribution to DT is 2 T0^2/X0 = 0.14307858749261432.
The orthogonal contribution is 18.80567087845467. The sum is
18.948749465947284, agreeing with the reported split.

Orthogonality here means orthogonality in the L2 space of vorticity fields.
It does not by itself mean disjoint Fourier support. A polynomial Gaussian
is not supported on finitely many Fourier modes. "Newly generated modes"
should be read as deformation of the initial profile unless a specific
spectral projection is defined. The reported centroid shift may provide
additional spectral information, but it is not reproduced in this audit.

5. Two different critical thresholds

For u(x,0) = a U(Kx), a = c nu K, with a,K,nu,c positive,
three-dimensional whole-space scaling gives

T(0) = a^3 T0,
X(0) = a^2 K^(-1) X0,
Y(0) = a^2 K Y0,

T'(0) = a^4 K C_E - 16 nu a^3 K^2 T0
      = nu^4 K^5 c^3 (c C_E - 16 T0).

Production increases initially exactly when

c > c_T = 16 T0/C_E = 3.894245215937192.

But the enstrophy identity is X'/2 = T - nu Y, and therefore

X'(0)/2 = nu^3 K^3 c^2 (c T0 - Y0).

Enstrophy increases initially exactly when

c > c_X = Y0/T0 = 3807 sqrt(6)/64 = 145.70636641836813.

In particular, throughout c_T < c < c_X, production is rising while
enstrophy is falling. At c = c_X, the first enstrophy derivative is zero;
this audit does not determine the next nonzero derivative there.
Neither threshold is a blow-up or regularity threshold.

6. Consequence for the next experiment

The packet verifies genuine initial nonlinear reinforcement. The available
margin remains an initial derivative and says nothing quantitative about
how long the reinforcing configuration persists. Smooth local evolution
provides continuity, but no controlled turnover-window lower bound is proved.

Use s = a K t and u = a v(Kx,s). Then
v_s = -P((v . grad)v) + c^(-1) Delta v, v(0) = U.

A useful next numerical probe would bracket both thresholds, for example
c = 3, 4, 100, 150, 200, over 0 <= s <= 1. Record T(v), Y(v), X(v),
the net balance G(s) = T(v) - Y(v)/c, and its cumulative integral.
Check X(s)-X(0) = 2 integral_0^s G(sigma) d sigma with domain and resolution
controls. This is a proposed experiment, not a run completed by this audit.

Any finite simulation remains numerical evidence. A result relevant to the
