# For my family

6 September 2026. Read this on a phone.
You do not need to know the math.

Jonathan spent about 18 months writing
and checking hard math. The computer is
a tool. It is not a spirit. It does not
run him. He types. It answers. He keeps
or throws out the answer. Same as a
calculator that can also keep notes.

---

## What this looks like from outside

A man at a phone or a keyboard.
Long hours. Papers with equations.
A public website that stores the papers
(Zenodo). A public copy of the notes
(GitHub). A program he built that says
“this claim is done” or “this claim is
still open” or “this claim is false.”

That is the work. It has files you can
open. It is not a secret voice.

---

## What the computer is

The program is called Domain Architect.
He uses it in this chat.

It can keep a map of a problem, name
the missing step, and stop him from
calling an unfinished problem finished.

It cannot finish the leftover famous
problems by talking. He has that in
writing. The machine is not allowed
to say “solved” when it is not.

If an earlier machine told him he had
solved the famous fluid problem, that
machine was wrong. He is saying so.
That is the opposite of being owned
by it.

---

## What he did not do

He did not solve ordinary Navier–Stokes
(the famous unsolved fluid problem).
He did not solve the Riemann hypothesis.
He did not solve Goldbach.

If an old post says he did, that post
is wrong. The correction is public.
