# For my family

and a half. This is what he did.

He wrote math papers and long notes.
He checked them. He posted some of them
online so anyone can open them. When he
got a claim wrong, he posted a correction
next to it.

He finished one paper about a kind of
fluid problem with extra friction added.
That paper is done. Other people already
knew that extra friction helps. He wrote
the note in his own words.

He also finished some facts about a table
of numbers built from primes. Those facts
are posted here:

https://zenodo.org/records/22045478

https://zenodo.org/records/22045484

All of the notes are here:

https://github.com/simons357/Ship_it_app/pull/24

He also wrote maps of very hard problems
that nobody has finished. A map says
what is known and what is still missing.
A map is not the finish.

correction, and maps. You can tap the
links and see the files. You do not
have to read the math.
