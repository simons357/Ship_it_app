## The couple

One universal pair:

\[
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=8\pi G\,T_{\mu\nu}.
\]

**What is coupled:** the metric (curvature) to stress-energy.
**The coupling constant:** \(G\) (or \(\kappa=\sqrt{8\pi G}\)).
**Universal:** every species with energy sources curvature
the same way. That is the equivalence principle. Gravity is
not a fourth Standard-Model gauge click.

Quantum fields, as we actually use them, couple to a
**classical** metric:

\[
S=S_{\mathrm{EH}}[g]+S_{\mathrm{matter}}[g,\psi,A].
\]

Geometry is not quantized in any working, complete theory
on this desk. The fields are. That semiclassical couple
**passes** (Hawking, inflation spectra). It is not a UV
unifier.

## The two leftovers

hitting:
