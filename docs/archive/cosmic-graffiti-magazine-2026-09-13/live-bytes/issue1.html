This week’s headlines belong on Cosmic Graffiti.

A reported swarm of roughly ten thousand concurrent agents claimed a finite-time singularity for three-dimensional Navier–Stokes with a smooth external force, alongside a Lean formalization. Coverage also says they are not claiming a prize, and that independent peers still verify the result.

That is a historically interesting moment for science — and for a magazine built on wonder first, labels loud, and room for people to talk.

One plain note on the math object

With respect to everyone working this problem: a result that includes a smooth external force is a specific mathematical setup. It is not automatically the same as the classical unforced regularity question people often picture when they hear “Navier–Stokes.” Naming that carefully is how we honor the work — theirs and everyone’s — without slogans eating the equation.
