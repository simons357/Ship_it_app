## What is open

- Canonical SFE: **unresolved**. Several historical formulas used the same
  letters. None is the equation.
- UHF and DHFA as physical laws: not established.
- A single picture that would join fluids, primes, and gravity: not in hand.
- Unconditional 3D Navier–Stokes, the Riemann hypothesis, and Goldbach: not
  claimed here.

I am still working that universe question at this desk. Open is the honest
state.

## What Domain Architect is for

Paste an equation. The auditor names roles, collisions, and what remains
untested. That is inquiry.

ChatVault is search. Same origin, not the same engine. Filing an FRA report
into the vault does not prove the report.

## What is not claimed

- No theory of everything.
- No “one Hamiltonian, three prizes.”
- No canonical Simons Field Equation.
- No Millennium closure from this software.
- Domain Architect does not prove theorems. It does not rank the vault.
