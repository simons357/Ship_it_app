# CV lines — teaching, sitting notes only

6 September 2026. What you can put on a
CV if you want to teach. Not a hiring
promise. Not a leftover close.

---

## What a teaching search actually reads

A high-school job reads license, subject
test, and hours in a room. Notes barely
move that.

A community-college or lecturer job reads
degree first, then teaching. Notes can sit
under working papers. They will not replace
a master’s or a classroom record.

A research-university math job reads
peer-reviewed papers in the field. These
notes will not make that search.

List sitting notes as working papers /
preprints. Do not list them as journal
articles. Do not list leftover closes.

---

Heading: Working papers / preprints

1. J. R. Simons. Global regularity for the
   Q1-augmented Navier–Stokes system (this
   PDE only). Zenodo, [year]. [DOI].
   One line under it: Records Ladyzhenskaya
   / p-Laplacian regularity in Q1 notation.
   Not classical 3D Navier–Stokes.

   Deposit first: docs/THEOREM-A-DEPOSIT.md
   Speech: docs/DA-ZENODO-SAY.md
   PDF: docs/THEOREM-A-Q1.pdf

2. J. R. Simons. [the August GCD title as
   it already stands on Zenodo]. Zenodo
   22045478, 2026.
   One line: Inverse-GCD / spectral matrix
   note. Not the Riemann hypothesis. Not
   Goldbach’s conjecture.

3. J. R. Simons. Errata / retraction note.
   Zenodo 22045484, 2026.
   One line: Withdraws the false full-Q
   floor and the false H_N floor. Keep this
   next to 22045478.

Optional, after the DOI exists, as a map
not a theorem:

4. J. R. Simons. Swirl, alignment, and the
   leftover in three-dimensional
   Navier-Stokes (a map, not a proof).
   Zenodo, [year]. [DOI].
   One line: Names the CF / BdVB leftover.
   Does not prove ordinary NS.

Optional, only if you write it as a process
note and not a theorem:

5. Domain Architect — process note
   (checker / map; does not prove NS or RH).

---

## Do not put these

Solved Navier–Stokes. Solved RH. BSD.
Hodge. Yang–Mills mass gap. P vs NP.
Goldbach’s conjecture. CosmoEvolution
16/16. DA as a solver. Axisymmetric
ordinary NS with swirl. “Millennium” or
prize language.

An honest short list beats a long fake
one. A search that checks Zenodo will
read the title. If the title says you
closed ordinary NS, that is the refuse.

Older deposits that overclaim: leave them
up, point to the errata, do not add them
as wins.

---

## How to write the rest of the CV

Education and (if you have it) a teaching
license or classroom hours go above these
notes. Software you built can go under
projects: Domain Architect as a checker,
not as a proof.

Prime Field Technologies LLC can go under
work. Do not use it as a journal name.
