# What we stick on the GRAFITTI pictures

The plates stay plates. We do **not** paint the 800-word cut onto the wall.

## How to use them. With what.

Open `apps/cosmic-grafitti/index.html` on a phone and swipe. Prev / Next sit under the rail. Arrow keys work on a desk.

**Use the pictures with:**

1. **The leftover print cut** — `docs/SWIRL-MAGAZINE.md`. That is the text. The picture gets one leftover name.
2. **A phone** — this is a flipbook, not a live site. GitHub Pages is off. AirDrop the folder, or open the file in Files / Safari.
3. **Short leftover tags** — wheatpaste cards on top of the art. The spray letters baked into the PNGs are atmosphere. Some of them are garbled. Trust the HTML cards.
4. **An editor sentence** — the Print spread. SIAM News commentary / letter, or any magazine that will take a dated essay. Not a research article that proves regularity.

**Do not use them with:** Clay Statement B, a proof, the May Cosmic Star Lattice essay, Tikkun, Saturn hexagon, Axis of Evil, “Planck confirms NS,” or “NS is solved.”
