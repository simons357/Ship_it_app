## What sits

Let \(u\in L^2(\mathbb{T}^3)\) be divergence-free and
mean-zero, \(\omega=\nabla\times u\). Biot–Savart on
this space is \(\widehat{u}(k)=ik\wedge\widehat{\omega}(k)/|k|^2\),
equivalently \(|\widehat{\omega}(k)|=|k|\,|\widehat{u}(k)|\).

**Lemma P1 (low-pass thinness).**
If \(\widehat{\omega}(k)=0\) whenever \(|k|>K\), then

\[
\int|\omega|^2
\le
K^2\int|u|^2.
\]

If also \(K\le C/\rho\),

\[
\rho^2\int|\omega|^2
\le
C^2\int|u|^2.
\]

Proof: Plancherel. The ratio is the energy-weighted
mean of \(|k|^2\), hence at most \(K^2\).

That is P1’s inequality, from Biot–Savart, on a
stated class. Not a picture. Not one Gaussian pair.
