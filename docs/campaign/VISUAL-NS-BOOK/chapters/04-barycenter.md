# 04 — This room: the barycenter

**Hero:** [`../../../ns-review/assets/lemma-campaign/lemma-star-barycenter.png`](../../../ns-review/assets/lemma-campaign/lemma-star-barycenter.png)

## Plain language

In frequency space, spin lives across big swirls and tiny swirls. The **barycenter** \(\Lambda\) is the center of mass of that spin. We are standing in that room: stretch vs spread is measured *around that center*, not around zero. That is “where we are” relative to the map — a locus in the construction, not a prize billboard.

## What the equation means (optional)

\[
\Lambda=\frac{Y}{X},\qquad
D_s=Z-\Lambda Y\ge 0,\qquad
T_c=-\langle B(v,v),A(A-\Lambda)v\rangle.
\]

- \(\Lambda\) — where the action is centered in frequency.  
- \(D_s\) — how smeared the spin is away from that center.  
- \(T_c\) — how hard nonlinear swirling pumps toward finer scales, measured relative to \(\Lambda\).

## Honesty

Being in the room means the bookkeeping is correctly centered. It does **not** mean the last door is open.
