# 06 — Shape is not size

**Hero:** [`../../../ns-review/assets/lemma-campaign/02-shape-ne-size.png`](../../../ns-review/assets/lemma-campaign/02-shape-ne-size.png)

## Plain language

Crank the amplitude of the whole field: the shape score is built so overall loudness cancels. What’s left is geometry among shells — relative arrangement of swirls, not how hard you turn the volume knob. Uniform Fourier dilation leaves the same score alone for the same reason.

## What the equation means (optional)

Under \(v\mapsto a v\), numerator and denominator of \(\mathcal{R}_\star\) scale the same way, so amplitude drops out. That is why we talk about **shape**, not raw size. A once-proposed universal product with the wrong amplitude scaling (\(|T_c|\le C\|v\|_2 X^{3/2}\)) is algebraically false — retired, not a live target.

## Honesty

Scale invariance of the score does not prove the score is bounded. Boundedness is the open door.
