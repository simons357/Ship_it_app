# 08 — What “blowup” means

**Hero:** [`../../../ns-review/assets/lemma-campaign/03-tug-of-war-stretch-vs-spread.png`](../../../ns-review/assets/lemma-campaign/03-tug-of-war-stretch-vs-spread.png)

## Plain language

Blowup ≠ explosion ≠ Big Bang. In PDEs it means some size (usually spin / gradients) goes to infinity in **finite time**, so the smooth mathematical solution cannot continue. Theoretical singularity of the equations — not a claim about the universe ending.

## What the equation means (optional)

Along strong solutions the spectral-shift identity

\[
\Lambda'=\frac{2}{X}\bigl(T_c-\nu D_s\bigr)
\]

tracks how the barycenter moves. That identity is bookkeeping. Establishing it does **not** establish the Lemma★ ratio bound, depletion, or regularity.

## Honesty

Explaining blowup carefully is education. It is not evidence that blowup happens — or that it doesn’t.
