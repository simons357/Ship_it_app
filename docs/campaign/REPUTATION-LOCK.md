# Reputation lock

Public posture for the proof journey and campaign face.

---

## What reputation is

Reputation sits on **full evidence** and a **clean chain**:

- Every major book is linkable (Lemma★, Φ-renorm, five-lane, Q6, SND, DA, KEEP DOIs, archive shelf).
- Open analytic needs are named as **math objects**, visible on the chain.
- Corrections live **under the content** (status index, audit notes, description errata) — not as scarlet billboards on titles or hero figures.

Mistakes are acceptable when they are corrected in place and the chain stays honest. Hiding gaps damages reputation more than an open estimate does.

---

## What reputation is not

- Not a verdict stamp (“we solved” / “we have not solved”).
- Not theater (dream-team cast lists, investigator biography framing, shade posts).
- Not collapsing adjacent books into one Millennium claim.

Experts read status from the chain. Campaign materials **show the construction**; they do not announce a prize outcome.

---

## Barycenter

The spectral **barycenter** figure is the central visual — “we are there” energy as a **locus in the construction**:

- scale \(\Lambda=Y/X\),
- spread \(D_s\),
- centered stretch \(T_c\),
- shape quotient \(\mathcal{R}_\star\).

It marks where the packaging lives. It is **not** a claim that Clay Statement (B) is closed, and campaign copy must not say “Clay solved,” “Millennium closed,” or the negated billboard forms either.
