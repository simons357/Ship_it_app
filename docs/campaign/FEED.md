# Daily feed — Substack + Skool

**Purpose:** Every day, put one thing we figured out in front of people. Pictures + plain language. Occasional human commentary from Jonathan; routine posts can be “robot” (AI desk) labeled honestly.

**Deal unchanged:** in the house, at the door, not upstairs. No Clay-closed / breakthrough trophy lines.

| Channel | Use for | Length |

## Daily rhythm (30–45 min)

1. Pick **one** figured-out chip from the queue (below).  
2. Attach **one** figure (or reuse barycenter/chain).  
3. Skool: short post.  
4. Every 2–3 days: Substack expands that chip into a mini-essay.  
5. Log the date in `FEED-LOG.md` so you don’t repeat blindly.

If nothing new closed mathematically that day: post a **map crumb** (definition, picture, “what blowup isn’t”). Consistency > novelty.

---

## Hard locks (paste at top of editorial brain)

- No “NS solved” / “imminent breakthrough” / “notify management.”  
- Blowup ≠ Big Bang (link FAQ).  
- Open door = product bound on \(T_c\) / \(\mathcal{R}_\star\) — named, not shamed.  
- Corrections under content, not in titles.  
- Kindness toward other people online — no shade threads.
