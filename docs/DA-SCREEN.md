# Screen: does anyone claiming unification actually pass?

`python3 scripts/da_machine.py screen`

Yes, it makes sense. DA can discern unification. There
are published claims. They get the same two-level check.
Do not glue the levels.

## The two levels

**gauge3.** Do \(\alpha_{\mathrm{em}}\), \(\alpha_s\), and
the weak mixing meet at one scale? This is a real,
fail-able claim. People have been running it for fifty
years.

**nature4.** One map \(F\) hits
\((g_s,g_w,g_{\mathrm{em}},g_N)\) and \(\Lambda\) stays in
the sum. That is the unifier-at-\(\varepsilon\) we have
been using. Stricter. Almost nobody even claims it.

A program can be **open as gauge3** and **fail as nature4**.
That is discernment, not a dodge.
