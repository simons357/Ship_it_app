## The idea (one breath)

**TITAN-X** is a free-flight **stratospheric aerostat** — helium buoyancy + solar power — meant to sit in the altitude band between conventional aircraft and satellites with a modular **~20,000 lb** payload. Same hull family; swap gondolas / underbelly modules by mission. Public materials lead civil utility first; **selected dual-use** configurations share that platform.
