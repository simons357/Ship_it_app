## The idea (one breath)

Use the same TITAN-X hull family as a **persistent on-station disaster node**: after an earthquake (or similar wide-area catastrophe), the platform stays overhead or nearby as a **floating hospital module** — several beds aboard to stabilize and treat victims — while also supporting observation, communications restoration, and logistics that ground hospitals may have lost.

Civil urgency first; dual-use relevance for units that care about disaster / homeland response and civil–military mutual support.

---

## Earthquake rescue use case (planning vignette)

1. **Shake / collapse** — roads, runways, and local hospitals damaged or overloaded.  
2. **Persistent loft** — a pre-positioned or rapidly tasked TITAN-X arrives / remains in the regional band (hours-scale framing from the parent plan pack — not a guaranteed ETA).  
3. **Overhead / near-site care** — gondola hospital module treats and holds casualties who cannot wait for distant surge hospitals.  
4. **Companion roles on the same node** — temporary comms, wide-area observation for search priority, drop / drone logistics for supplies (concept; effector/drone packages are modular and can be outsourced).  
5. **Handoff** — stabilize → evacuate when ground corridors reopen; module can remain as a persistent care / command node during aftershock periods.

This is a **planning story**, not an after-action claim from a past deployment.
