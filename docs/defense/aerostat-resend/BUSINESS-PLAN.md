## 1. Vision

Build persistent aerial infrastructure in the band between aircraft and satellites: a free-flight stratospheric aerostat with indefinite solar/buoyancy endurance and a 20,000 lb modular payload — pre-positioned near cities and high-risk regions so response is measured in hours, not days.

---

## 2. The product

| Layer | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Hull | Swept manta planform, ~480 ft × 200 ft |
| Lift / power | Helium buoyancy + solar for continuous station-keeping |
| Altitude | 500 – 130,000 ft (tree-top to lower stratosphere) |
| Payload | 20,000 lb net; gondolas and underbelly modules swap by mission |
| Ops carbon | Near-zero for persistent loiter (no combustion for station-keeping) |
| Special | Rockoon loft to ~100,000 ft, then recover the platform |

---

## 3. Go-to-market (regional deployment)

1. **Pathfinder validation** — prove endurance, modular swap, and station-keeping.  
2. **First full-scale hull** — certify the operational unit class.  
3. **Initial regional nodes** — major metros / high-value regions (one unit or a pair).  
4. **California firefighting concentration** — sensors + retardant/water + incident command.  
5. **Fleet scale-up** — civil/commercial utilization underwrites year-round availability; smaller complementary platforms fill secondary roles.

---

## 4. Revenue / utilization logic

Civil and commercial utilization is intended to help underwrite the cost of keeping assets available year-round. Mission mix includes disaster/wildfire, infrastructure monitoring, emergency communications, maritime awareness, public-safety drone awareness, science observation, rockoon services, and selected dual-use defense configurations — all on one modular cost base.

---

## 5. Economics (planning)

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Unit CAPEX (volume) | $13.0M |
| 12-unit fleet CAPEX | $156M |
| Annual OPEX / unit | $2.4M |
| Target break-even | ~$200K / month per unit |
| Planning 10-year fleet ROI | 17.8 : 1 |

Capital is staged; do not present volume CAPEX as the pathfinder price.

---

## 6. Risks & guardrails

- Treat CAPEX/OPEX/ROI as **planning estimates**, not contracted performance.  
- Firefighting modules are one configuration — do not imply a separate single-mission fleet.  
- Dual-use defense options share the civil platform story; lead with regional civil utility.  
- Helium, airspace, and certification are pathfinder gates before fleet claims.

---

## 7. One-sentence plan

**Pre-position modular stratospheric TITAN-X units as persistent regional infrastructure — starting with city nodes and a California wildfire layer — so the same hull family covers disaster, communications, observation, rockoon, and selected defense missions on a shared cost base.**
