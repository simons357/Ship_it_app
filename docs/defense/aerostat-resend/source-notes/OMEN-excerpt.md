### OMEN
**Defense · Dual-Use (commercial + scientific)** · Persistent Spectral Surveillance Aerostat

OMEN is a tethered aerostat platform carrying a Q6 spectral sensor payload for persistent intelligence, surveillance, and weather / anomaly reconnaissance — work a short-endurance drone cannot hold, and a satellite cannot stare at continuously.

**Dual-use framing:** commercial logistics / disaster response and scientific atmospheric studies — not a single-purpose weapons narrative.

#### What fills the empty middle (required companion app)
OMEN is incomplete without the paired operator app:

1. **Anomaly lock** — Over the treeline, the detection system locks onto a target / anomaly track.  
2. **Decision clock** — Displays time remaining until suggestions are no longer actionable (intervention window closes).  
3. **Counter-UAS lane** — App workflow to identify and defeat hostile / unauthorized drones in the protected volume.  
4. **Friendly drone logistics** — Prepacked disaster drones for supply delivery with longer hover / loiter profiles for last-mile relief.

#### Platform stack
- **Platform:** Tethered dirigible / aerostat — persistent station-keeping, low RF signature design target  
- **Sensor:** Q6 passive spectral analyzer — atmospheric, electromagnetic, acoustic  
- **Capability targets:** Weather / anomaly detection, ISR support, comms-denied operations, autonomous drone routing support, disaster resupply loiter  
- **Dual-use:** Forward operating base protection **and** commercial / scientific / disaster relief (grid-failure early warning, wildfire detection, flood prediction, prepacked supply drones)
