6 September 2026. Plain English.

and asking: what would that method try, and
what can it not give. Papers, not a phone

## Ordinary NS (the one you want)

Those papers name the same hole: one bound
that works for every starting fluid, not a
computer-box reading.

- A paper on extra stress (Ladyzhenskaya)
  already closed a different equation.
  It cannot be imported onto ordinary NS.
- Alignment “if the swirl stays lined up”
  is still an if, not a proof for every fluid.
- The middle-strain piece looked calm on
  one computer box. A box is not every fluid.
- A regularized cousin is a different
  equation. Averaging into a cousin is not
  the ordinary problem.

So the shot pointed at the hole. It did
not fill the hole.

## RH

Those papers name the same hole: every
zeta zero on the 1/2 line.

- The functional equation is a symmetry,
  not that statement.
- The prime-number theorem keeps zeros off
  the line Re = 1. It does not push all the
  way to 1/2.
- Infinitely many zeros on the line is not
  all zeros.
- A positive share on the line is not all.
- Your GCD / matrix paper is not a zero.

So the shot pointed at the hole. It did
not fill the hole.
