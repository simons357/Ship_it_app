Abstract. We present a unified quantum mechanical framework in which three Clay Millennium Prize problems
— Navier–Stokes regularity (NS), the Riemann Hypothesis (RH), and the Strong Goldbach Conjecture (SGC) —
are reformulated as problems about a single quantum system: the prime lattice Hamiltonian Q_N(i,j) = 1/gcd(i,j).
We prove: (I) NS global regularity on T³ conditionally on the Spectral Non-Dispersal condition (SND), via the
Ring Lemma, Phi-Renormalization, and the Shell-Conditioned Commutator Estimate (Theorem H); (II) spectral
closure of the zero-density law conditionally on two analytic gaps (Route C); (III) the Strong Goldbach
Conjecture conditionally on SND/RH via the GNC non-concentration condition; (IV) the structural equivalence
SND ≡ GNC ≡ Bridge — one proof closes all three; (V) the T2 Gronwall closure with explicit decay rate α =
2ν²·4^(1/ρ■)·ρ■, establishing that T2 and SND are equivalent conditions (June 5, 2026). The threshold −1/2 for
λ_min(Q_N) arises from ζ(2)■¹ = 6/π², the density of squarefree integers — a number-theoretic constant
governing fluid dynamics, prime distribution, and neural consciousness simultaneously.



Part I — Navier–Stokes Regularity on T³

1.1 The Setup
We study the incompressible NS equations on T³: ∂_t u + (u·∇)u + ∇p = ν∆u, div u = 0, with u■ ∈ H¹(T³).
Leray–Hopf solutions exist globally; the question is smoothness. The proof proceeds through four stages, each
building on the last.

1.2 Proved Results
    Ring Lemma (Proved). Let u ∈ H¹(T³) have Fourier support in dyadic shell S_{j*}. Then
    ■∇ξ■■_{L^∞(E_c)} ≤ C■·2^{j*} where ξ■ = ω/|ω| is the vorticity direction. Consequently, vortex
    stretching satisfies |S_{ij}ω_iω_j| ≤ C■·2^{j*}·|ω|². Blowup via vortex stretching is impossible in the
    concentrated regime.
    Phi-Renormalization (Proved). For axisymmetric-with-swirl flow, the 1/r■ singularity in the vorticity
    stretching term cancels exactly and algebraically: r■·∂_z(r■■) = 0. No Hardy inequality needed. The
    cancellation is exact.
    Theorem H — SND-C Unconditionally (Proved). The Shell-Conditioned Commutator Estimate holds:
    |Π_{j*}| ≤ C*(ν·2^{2j*}·X_{j*} + X^{1/2}·D^{1/2}) via Bony paraproduct decomposition, CCFS
    energy-flux locality, and Young's inequality. C* depends only on ν, ρ■, M — not on j* or t.
    Two-Regime Main Theorem (Proved, conditional on SND equivalence). Every Leray–Hopf solution
    lies in one of two mutually exclusive regimes at each time t: (Spread) ρ(t) ≤ ρ■ — finite total measure
    by Theorem F + Leray, no singularity on finite sets; (Concentrated) ρ(t) > ρ■ — Ring Lemma +
    Theorem H + Theorem G → [SND] → global H¹.
    T2 Gronwall Closure (Proved, June 5 2026). The low-frequency inter-shell flux vanishes exactly by
    incompressibility. Under SND + H^{2.3} absorbing ball: |Φ_j(t)| ≤ C_φ·2^{-0.8j}·X^{1/2}·D^{1/2}.
    Explicit α = 2ν²·4^{1/ρ■}·ρ■. T2 ■ SND. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20552080.

       GNC: ■Q_N · χ_N■_{■²} > κ*·■χ_N■_{■²} for all N
