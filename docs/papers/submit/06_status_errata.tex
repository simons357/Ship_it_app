stack I still claim, and which public claims I withdraw. It is not a
prize announcement. Unconditional global regularity for classical
three-dimensional Navier--Stokes is not claimed. The Riemann Hypothesis
and the Goldbach conjecture are not claimed.

\section*{On Q6}

The inverse-GCD / ``Q6'' operator is a real matrix construction.
The prize narrative built on it --- that Q6 proves Goldbach, RH, or
Navier--Stokes, or that a full-spectrum Bridge floor holds --- does
\emph{not} stand. The surviving Q6-adjacent result is Bridge$^*$ only
(single prime-pair Rayleigh bound). Phi--Q6 ``correspondence'' is
withdrawn as a load-bearing claim.

\section*{Break, not erasure}

I am pausing this research line to work on products and anesthesia
writing. The corrected Tier~1 notes are the public credit I want
attached to my name. Older overclaim records remain as dated archive
with this errata.
