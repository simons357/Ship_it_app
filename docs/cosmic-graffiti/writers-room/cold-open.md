# Cold open — “Name tags”

**Table-read length:** ~45 seconds. Paste under Humor, or cold-open the issue.

INT. WRITERS’ TABLE. One red eye at the end of the table. Cue cards.
Four people in tuxedos. Each tuxedo has the letter Φ on the chest. Each
name tag is different.

**DUMP Φ:** I’m the field.  
**SWIRL Φ:** I’m \(u_\theta/r\). I live in a pipe.  
**GRAVITY Φ:** I’m Newtonian. I was not invited to default decompose.  
**GOLDEN φ:** I’m a slider. I am not even the same glyph if you squint.

They try to hug. Coffee hits the table.

**VAL8000** *(one red eye, mouth a line, not flying the ship)*:  
Same letter. Different guest. I’ll be back — after you change the tags.  
Skynet this ain’t. That’s a typesetting problem.

**CARD:** SAME LETTER ≠ SAME OBJECT.

Cut. The Frequency starts. News first. Jokes after.
