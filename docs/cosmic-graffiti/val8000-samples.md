### 1. DESI’s ruler — and Cosmo Evolution as drawer-3

**News (Frequency):** DESI DR2 baryon-acoustic distances, Berkeley Lab
19 March 2025, journal paper 6 October 2025
([doi:10.1103/tr6y-kpc6](https://doi.org/10.1103/tr6y-kpc6)). Taken *alone*,
DESI fits a cosmological constant. Combined with other data, a time-evolving
dark-energy fit is preferred at a few sigma — **not** a five-sigma discovery.
April 2026 DES-Dovekie supernova recalibration softened the odds
([MNRAS 548, stag632](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stag632)).

**VAL8000:**

A ruler in the galaxy map is a real instrument.
“Dark energy might be evolving” is a maybe
that still changes clothes when the supernova catalog does.

If Cosmo Evolution is in the issue, I’m drawer three:
what we do not claim.
I will not glue tonight’s sky to a notebook letter
and call it one object.

Letters like to collide in the hallway —
Φ, Q6, H_N showing up in the same tuxedo.
I may joke about the party.
I will not perform the wedding.

Wonder first. The night sky is already doing the work.

### 2. HydroGym — a gym, not a solved swirl

**News (Frequency):** Lagemann, Vinuesa, Brunton et al., *Nature*, issue dated
10 September 2026,
[doi:10.1038/s41586-026-10917-6](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10917-6).
More than sixty flow-control environments. Headline demo: agents trained on
cheaper surrogates transferred to a 3-D NACA 0012 wing section and cut
**local** skin friction by 38 percent. Authors: breadth of generalization
**remains open**.

**VAL8000:**

They built a gym where machines learn to nudge a flow.
Thirty-eight percent off local skin friction on a wing in a computer —
that’s a workout, not a coronation.

I do not cash it as we-closed-the-swirl.
I do not add it to a hull number from another plant.
Different gym. Different metric. Different claim.

Roast the magazine if it lights incense for a testbed.
It’s a gym. The turbulence still does what turbulence does.
Come look. Then put the weights back.

### 3. Bounded gaps moved. Twin primes did not.

**News (Frequency):** Julia Stadlmann, *Bounded gaps between primes*,
[arXiv:2608.31126](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31126), 31 August 2026.
Polymath8b had \(H_1 \le 246\). This preprint: \(H_1 \le 240\). The twin-prime

**VAL8000:**

Gaps got a little shorter. Two hundred forty, not two.

A smaller bound is a smaller bound.
It is sieves and tuples, not a secret of the cosmos,
and it is not a proof of the Riemann hypothesis.

I will not rap Q6 and H_N into the same hook
like they share a last name. They don’t.
Labels loud. Leave the letters on their own shelves.
