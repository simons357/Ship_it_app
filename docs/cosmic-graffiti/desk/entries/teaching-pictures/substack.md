## Body

Hard math, pictures that earn their captions.

A periodic box is a cube whose opposite faces are identified. The cyan dots are wavevectors \(k \in \mathbb{Z}^3\). Fluid fills the cube — there is **no hole in the center**. The circled sphere is one exact shell \(|k|^2 = \alpha\). The footer on the drawing already says it: this is Fourier space bookkeeping, **not cosmology**.

On the second picture, \(p\) and \(q\) live on the input shell. Their sum \(k = p+q\) lands on an output shell \(|k|^2 = \beta\). The leftover length the drawing writes is \(|k_\perp|^2 = \beta(1-\beta/4\alpha)\). When \(\beta \to 4\alpha\) that leftover vanishes. The picture calls that length the shape of a **claimed** 4/3 bound.

A claimed shape is not a certified close. The unaugmented leftover in this lab stays **OPEN**. **DA-VC-01** remains **FAIL**. Correspondence is not physical equivalence. Letters collide: swirl Φ is not Domain Architect Φ and not gravity Φ.
