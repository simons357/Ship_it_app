## Body

This week’s headlines belong on Cosmic Graffiti.

A reported swarm of roughly **ten thousand concurrent agents** claimed a finite-time singularity for three-dimensional Navier–Stokes **with a smooth external force**, alongside a Lean formalization. Coverage also says they are **not** claiming a prize, and that **independent peers still verify** the result.

That is a historically interesting moment for science — and for a magazine built on wonder first, labels loud, and room for people to talk.

So: **Issue 1** is not a fight. It is an acknowledgment. The biggest story on our desk right now. Almost made for this magazine.

### What the reports say — Reported · not DA-certified

- About ~10,000 concurrent agents
- Claim: finite-time singularity for 3D NS *with smooth forcing*
- A Lean formalization claimed
- Forced singularity setup (smooth external force)
- They will not claim a prize
- Independent peers still verify

We do not rubber-stamp external claims here. Curiosity first. Respect for everyone doing the work. Peer process is the filter.

### One plain note on the math object

A result that includes a **smooth external force** is a specific mathematical setup. It is not automatically the same as the classical unforced regularity question people often picture when they hear “Navier–Stokes.” Naming that carefully is how we honor the work.
