Description (paste):

This note records global regularity for one
augmented equation: Navier-Stokes plus a
Ladyzhenskaya / p-Laplacian stress Q1, with
epsilon greater than zero and beta at least
1/2, on the 3-torus.

I overclaimed on earlier Zenodo records.
Those records stay up with this correction:
they do not close classical 3D Navier-Stokes,
they do not close the Riemann hypothesis,
and they do not close BSD. Inverse-GCD (Q)
is a prototype. Route C is conditional.

What I finally have is this PDE, not the
unaugmented problem. Uniform H1 as epsilon
goes to zero is not claimed. Classical
smoothness and existence is still open.

Credits: Ladyzhenskaya 1968/1969;
Malek-Necas-Ruzicka 1996. This is that class
in Q1 notation, not a new existence theory.
