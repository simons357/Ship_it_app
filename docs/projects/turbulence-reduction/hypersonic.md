This slot is the high-speed atmospheric study after ships. It is empty
on purpose. “HS missiles” in ordinary speech maps **here** as a flow
regime (compressibility, heat, plasma), not as a munition spec.

## Why it is not the ship stack

Ship riblets are sized to seawater \(u_\tau\). Hypersonic external flow
brings shock / boundary-layer interaction, wall temperature, and often a
plasma sheath. Copying \(s^+=15\)–\(17\) in micrometres from a hull film
is not a translation. Survival of any coating is the first constraint.

Plasma actuators appear in public hypersonic-transition papers. That is
**not** a DA-selected Cf mechanism and is **not** a weaponization path.

8–12% net Cf is **not awarded** in this slot. Heat and material limits
can erase a viscous-cut that looks plausible in incompressible oil.
