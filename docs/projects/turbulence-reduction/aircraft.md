## Why it is not the ship stack

Ship riblets are sized to seawater \(u_\tau\) and a fouling-release
interval. Transport aircraft at cruise are compressible, painted, weight-
and certification-limited. Small UAVs sit in mixed laminar/turbulent
patches at much lower \(Re\). A 50–90 µm cargo-ship groove is the wrong
scale for both.
