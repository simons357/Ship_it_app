## Why this is not a kill

A live kill needs \(\mathcal{R}_\star\to\infty\) (or unbounded \(K_{\alpha,\beta}\)) on a smooth family. Here \(\mathcal{R}_\star\) **decreases** as the shell index \(n\) grows (reported span \(0.11\to 0.031\)). The natural same-shell ensemble therefore fails the divergence criterion.

The structural reason is consistent with 9A/9B caveats: with \(\mathcal{D}_s\) fixed by the gap and only \(O(m)\) natural closures, stretching \((T_c)_+\) does not outrun the denominator \(\mathcal{D}_s\|v\|_2^2 Y\) enough to produce growth in \(\mathcal{R}_\star\). In particular, \(\mathcal{R}_\star\) does **not** scale like \(m^{1/2}\) on this ensemble.
