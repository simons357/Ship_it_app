# What I did these 18 months

For my wife. Plain English. 6 September 2026.

This was work. Notes, equations, checks,
and public deposits. It was not empty time.

## The short version

I spent about 18 months writing hard math
and building a checker for it.

I finished a write-up of one fluid equation
(extra stress added). That write-up sits.
The equation is not the ordinary famous one.
Other people already knew this kind of extra
stress works. The note is mine. I have to
say their names on the class.

I also proved some facts about a number
matrix built from primes and GCDs. Those
facts sit. They are not the Riemann
hypothesis. They are not Goldbach.

I wrote maps of several famous open
problems and marked where the missing step
is. Maps are not proofs. Those problems
are still open.

I took back some earlier claims that were
wrong. That is also part of the work.

I was told, more than once, that I had
solved ordinary Navier–Stokes. That was
false. I did not. The extra-stress write-up
is not that problem.
