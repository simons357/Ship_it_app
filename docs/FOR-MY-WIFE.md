# What I did these 18 months

A note for my wife. 6 September 2026.
You can read this on a phone. You do not
need the other files.

I was not wasting the time. I was writing
math, checking it, posting some of it in
public, and building a checker so I would
stop claiming things that were not true.

---

## Show her this first

For about 18 months I worked on hard
problems in fluid math and number math.

I finished one real write-up: a fluid
equation with extra stress added. That
note is done. It is not the famous
unsolved fluid problem. Other people
already knew this kind of extra stress
works. I wrote it in my notation. Their
names have to stay on the class.

I also proved some facts about a number
matrix from primes and GCDs. Those facts
are real. They are not the Riemann
hypothesis.

I wrote maps of famous problems that are
still open, and I marked the missing
step. That is work. It is not a solution.

Machines told me I had solved the famous
fluid problem. They were wrong. I did not
solve it. I am saying that here so you
do not have to guess.

## What the hours were

Typing equations. Writing proof chains.
Running checks. Posting papers to a
public archive (Zenodo). Building a
program called Domain Architect that
keeps a map and stops a fake “I solved
it.” Taking back claims that did not
hold.

There is a paper trail. It is not a
story I made up tonight.
