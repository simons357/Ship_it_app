# Simons Medical Innovations

**Founder:** Jonathan Simons, CRNA · Savannah, GA

## What we do

Invent airway and anesthesia tools from real OR problems—safer intubation workflows, lower contamination risk, fewer unnecessary steps.

## Why us

- 25+ years clinical CRNA practice  
- 20+ years airway device research  
- Inventor: laryngoscope with disposable blade cover (US 2010/0191061 A1)  
- Former owner, Winchester Anesthesia, LLC (2007–2025)

## Focus

1. **Airway safety** — devices clinicians will actually use in a difficult airway  
2. **Contamination control** — cleaner tools without slowing first-pass intent  
3. **Clinician usability** — if it adds cognitive load, it isn’t ready
