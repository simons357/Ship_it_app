# **QStack**

*A spectral diagnostics & correction platform — turning noisy signals into coherence, instability, and decision-support scores.*

**What it is**

QStack is a layered software engine that ingests messy, multi-domain data — optical measurements, fluid-flow simulations, time-series, sensor streams — and outputs three things engineers can act on: a **coherence score**, an **instability score**, and a measurable **correction** (lower error against a baseline). It is the productized, engineering-facing layer of Prime Field’s underlying spectral-mathematics research.

**The three-in-one stack**

| Layer | Role | Output |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Q6 / Spectral Floor | Research engine — detects concentration, rank collapse, hidden structure | Diagnostics, benchmarks |
| QStack (product layer) | Converts noisy inputs into coherence & instability scores | Dashboards, APIs, reports |
| E8 Substrate | Educational + longer-horizon lattice-coded routing / photonic testbed | Visualization, credibility front door |

**The commercial wedge: RMSE optics correction**

The near-term proof is deliberately narrow and **measurable**: adaptive optical-overlay correction. Raw measured points vs. QStack-corrected points, reported as a percent RMSE improvement against a baseline. No metaphysics required — the demo either lowers the error or it doesn’t, and that number is the sales pitch.

**Why now**

* Sensing is exploding across every product line — QStack is the layer that makes noisy sensor data trustworthy.
* The optics/overlay demo produces a hard, defensible benchmark that a partner or investor can verify.
* The same engine generalizes to fluid instability (NAV-42), biosignals (SpectraLock), and manufacturing metrology.

**Status & the ask**

**Stage:** pre-revenue, prototype/roadmap phase. Educational E8 explorer built; RMSE optics simulator specified. **Ask:** a design partner with real optical-overlay or metrology data to run the first head-to-head benchmark, and the resources to file narrow IP around the correction engine once the baseline improvement is confirmed.

# **QStack — How it works**

**Architecture**

QStack is a pipeline. Raw inputs pass through a spectral-operator front end (the Q6 / spectral-floor layer), which builds features from eigenvalue behavior and detects rank collapse and concentration. Those features feed a diagnostics-and-correction layer that emits coherence, instability, and correction fields. The E8 substrate sits alongside as a codebook / routing model and educational visualization.

**The RMSE correction equation**

The immediate engineering claim is error reduction, computed as:

RMSE_corrected = sqrt( (1/N) · Σᵢ || yᵢ − ( yᵢ_raw + α·∇Hᵢ + β·∇Cᵢ + γ·∇Dᵢ ) ||² )

where *yᵢ* is the ground-truth optical point, *yᵢ_raw* the measured point before correction, *H* a harmonic-alignment field, *C* a coherence score, and *D* a distortion/dispersion term. The product target is not to prove the physics of H, C, D — only to show they reduce RMSE against a baseline, reported as %ΔRMSE = 100 · (RMSE_raw − RMSE_corrected) / RMSE_raw.

**E8 recursive substrate**

A recursive exceptional-lattice network whose local cells are E8-root-inspired point clouds and whose global structure branches like a neural hierarchy — generated by:

Xₙ₊₁ = ⋃_{x ∈ Xₙ} ( x + sⁿ · Oₓ · Φ(E8) )

Presented as a visualization and testbed for packing, spectral richness, and hierarchical routing — not as a new proven Lie-group result. Effects to test: persistent spectral gaps, higher effective rank than flat kernels, lower collision rates than random codebooks, and natural error-correction under missing nodes.
