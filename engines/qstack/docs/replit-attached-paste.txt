Abstract
We introduce a resonance-based regularization of the 3D incompressible Navier-Stokes equations. The framework uses a static resonant attractor (defined below) and its dynamic extension via the QStack operator (prime phase modulation, harmonic shells, and coherence viscosity). This produces dissipative dynamics that drive solutions toward smooth equilibria. We establish uniform energy bounds, vorticity control, and exponential convergence to the attractor, providing a constructive approach to global regularity.
1. The Resonant Attractor
The core idea is that the flow relaxes toward a family of smooth, bounded resonant states. These equilibria arise from a harmonic structure involving primes and weighted shells (full definition in Appendix A).

4. Bootstrap to Global Smoothness
Bounded energy + bounded vorticity + exponential attraction to smooth resonant equilibria, combined with standard parabolic regularity tools (enabled by the shared linear structure with classical Navier-Stokes), imply that solutions remain globally C^\infty for all t > 0.

Conclusion
The QStack framework provides a new resonance-based mechanism for controlling the nonlinear stretching term in 3D Navier-Stokes. Prime discretization and coherence viscosity together drive the system toward smooth bounded states.
