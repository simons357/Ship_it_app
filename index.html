What “coherence” means here

Coherence is a measurable alignment score among coupled parts of a system. When it holds, those parts stay jointly stable; when it drops, instability is already starting — often before failure is obvious.

  • Score

    Coherence index

    A single number for how aligned the coupled parts are right now (maritime: vessels · routes · environment).

  • Trend

    Decoherence rate

    How fast that alignment is falling apart — the early-warning slope.

  • Lead time

    Time-to-instability

    Estimated window before the score crosses a risk floor.

  • Impact

    Scenario delta → money

    Change a variable, watch coherence move, forecast financial impact — finance app and Harmonic Insights lane.
