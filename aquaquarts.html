Origin

Finland was the plan. Lithia Springs changed the water.

The path was going overseas — Finland — to see if we could ship something special here. Then the springs. Lithia Springs, Cherokee country. Lithium in the local water story. It changed how we think about water: not imported prestige alone, but place, mineral heritage, and a vessel worth keeping.

Experience and origin only. No health claims. Process black-boxed until filing.

Making

Decanter partnership

We are exploring production with Cherokee artisans in Cherokee country — shared costs, shared rewards — so the vessel is crafted with the same care as the water story. Win-win: makers build the heirloom; guests keep it.

Nothing here claims a signed Nation endorsement. It is an open invitation to build the decanter together.
