WHAT THE WILD IS SAYING · A NOTE FOR SAVANNAH

A field instrument. One old story a day. Not a city app.

Jonathan Simons. Wilmington Island — Chatham County, next to Savannah. 26 October 2026. This is a handout. It is not a partnership. The City seal does not belong on the product.

What it is

Phones on a porch and in a park. One can stay still. One can walk. The original sound stays on the phone. If it sounds like people, Listener does not write it down and does not guess who it was. UNKNOWN is allowed. We do not invent animals to look busy.

Someone real can stand in front of a green screen and read an old story, or teach a short lesson in listening. The older and rarer, the better.

What it is not

Not surveillance. Not 911. Not cameras. Not a tourist species map. Not the fake Savannah-marsh demo with Air / Water / Party and invented birds.

The house is not inside City of Savannah limits. A city conversation still matters for parks, recreation, and film. County matters for the island. One office cannot promise the other.

The small ask

A conversation, not a contract. No press release. No exclusive marsh.

  • City Recreation & Leisure — one listening hour with children at a community center: a story, a short lesson, then sit still. 1301 E. Victory Drive. 912-351-3841.
  • Neighborhood Parks / Park & Tree — listen in a public park with phones. No invented animal pins. No filming strangers.
  • Film & Tourism — only if someone films the children’s reading in the city. tourism@savannahga.gov · 912-351-3837.
  • Chatham County — the same listening ask for unincorporated parks near Wilmington Island.

Do not ask for cameras, plates, emergency systems, or a U.S. Fish & Wildlife research permit. That is later, if it is ever real.

What we will not do

  • Transcribe people or name speakers
  • Invent wildlife or fake Field Coherence numbers
  • Put the house on a public map
