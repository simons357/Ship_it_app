LISTENER

Privacy

LISTENER is private by default. Exact home or property coordinates, raw Wander routes, precise node positions, and private media stay on your device unless you take a separate action.

Location

We use location to mark Session Start, draw your breadcrumb, and run RETURN as a safety aid. Contributed locations are coarsened unless you explicitly elect otherwise.

Microphone

Recordings are originals kept on device. LISTENER does not transcribe speech and does not identify speakers. Probable human speech is excluded from wildlife encounters and cannot enter the Signal Library.

Sharing and contributing

Sharing a Listener Card is not contributing the original to the common library. Contribution is opt-in and wildlife-only.
