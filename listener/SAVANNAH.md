# LISTENER — for someone who can reach government in Savannah

Owner: Jonathan Simons  
Home: Wilmington Island (unincorporated Chatham County), Savannah area  
Date: 26 October 2026  

This page is a handout. It is not a city partnership. It does not put the City seal on the product. It does not put government on the first screen of the app.

## One sentence

Listener is a wildlife-only field instrument: what the wild is saying. It also reads one little story a day, and someone real can teach listening.

## What it is

Phones on a porch and in a park. One stays still. One can walk. The original sound stays on the phone. If it sounds like people talking, Listener does not write it down and does not guess who it was. UNKNOWN is allowed. We do not invent animals to look busy.

There is a green-screen page so a parent or guest can stand in front of a screen and read an old story, or teach a short lesson in listening. The older and rarer, the better.

## What it is not

Not a surveillance tool. Not a 911 feed. Not a camera network. Not a species tourist map. Not Claude’s Savannah-marsh demo (Air / Water / Party, fake coherence numbers, invented clapper rails). Not an official City of Savannah app.

Home is on Wilmington Island. That is Chatham County, not City of Savannah limits. A city contact still matters for parks, recreation, film, and anything inside the city. County matters for the island. Do not ask one office to promise the other.

## The small ask

Ask for a conversation, not a contract.

If the person can reach **City of Savannah**:

1. **Recreation & Leisure Services** (community centers, after-school, 1301 E. Victory Drive, 912-351-3841) — try one listening hour with children: a story, a short lesson, then sit still. No species quiz.
2. **Neighborhood Parks / Park & Tree** — permission to listen in a public park with phones. No invented animal pins. No filming strangers.
3. **Special Events, Film & Tourism** (1 Waring Drive, tourism@savannahga.gov, 912-351-3837) — only if someone films the green-screen reading in the city. That is a film question, not a tourism map.

If the person can reach **Chatham County**:

4. The same listening ask for unincorporated parks near Wilmington Island. That is where the house actually is.

Do not ask yet for a press release, a refuge research permit, exclusive marsh rights, or any access to cameras, plates, or emergency systems.
