## Description

LISTENER is a field instrument for the non-human world.

Leave one iPhone as your still Base. Take another as Scout. LISTENER records what the microphones hear, marks where you started, and draws the trail back. RETURN is a safety aid — not a replacement for a map you already trust.

Wildlife only. If something sounds like human speech, LISTENER does not transcribe it, does not guess who is speaking, and does not add it to the wildlife record.

UNKNOWN is a real result. We do not invent animal names to make the field look busy.

You can share a Listener Card without sending the original recording. Contributing to the Signal Library is a separate yes. Public location is coarse unless you choose otherwise.

Field Coherence (DA) is a real instrument. Until two nearby Listeners are actually synchronized, it shows an em dash and INSUFFICIENT FIELD DATA. We will not decorate a fake number.

People can watch a Broadcast without joining the field. Adding a phone as a Listener node is opt-in.
