# How to cite

Prefer the **Zenodo DOI** for each paper (permanent).

Example:

> Simons, J. R. (2026). *The Q_N Operator: Self-Adjointness, Spectral Floor, and a Route to the Riemann Hypothesis via Renormalized GCD Eigenvalues*. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20405591

Full author record:  
https://zenodo.org/search?q=creators.name%3A%22Simons%2C%20Jonathan%22
