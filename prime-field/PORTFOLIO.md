### Lean Experimental Design — Minimum Credible Test
**Falsification First · Budget-Gated Validation**

PFT does not stop at proposing a theory or device. We design the least expensive experiment capable of giving a trustworthy answer—including the control condition, measured endpoint, failure criterion, instrumentation, sample size, and analysis plan.

1. **Stage 0 — Desktop:** literature, prior art, dimensional analysis, simulation, and reproducible computation  
2. **Stage 1 — Bench:** off-the-shelf sensors, calibration standards, blinded controls, and the cheapest physical model that preserves the mechanism  
3. **Stage 2 — Pilot:** predeclared endpoints, uncertainty bounds, stop/go thresholds, and an independent replication package  
4. **Stage 3 — Scale:** prototype, prospective clinical or field study, regulatory planning, and manufacturing only after the earlier gates survive  

The objective is not the cheapest demonstration. It is the cheapest experiment that can honestly prove the device works—or tell us early that it does not.
