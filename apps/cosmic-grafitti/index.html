The cut that sits, as an if

Hölder 1/2 is their theorem.

In three-dimensional incompressible flow, vortex stretching is the term that can make vorticity grow.

Constantin–Fefferman 1993. If the direction of vorticity does not turn too fast where the vorticity is large — Lipschitz control of the angle — the solution stays smooth.

Those papers are theorems as ifs. They do not estimate the pairs they refused.

Do not merge letters

A cylinder still needs more.

Flux through the skin, at energy level, is Caffarelli–Kohn–Nirenberg as a smallness criterion, not as an a priori from finite energy. Exterior Biot–Savart is why localization papers exist.

After a closed local enstrophy budget, the right implication is Serrin’s criterion on a smaller cylinder. That implication is not a substitute for (6).

If (6) were proved and the skin flux were still open, the cylinder would still be open. If the cylinder is already small in the CKN sense, (6) was never needed on that cylinder.
