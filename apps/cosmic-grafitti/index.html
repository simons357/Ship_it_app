The refused pairs

In three-dimensional flow, vortex stretching can make vorticity grow. Constantin and Fefferman (1993) showed that if the direction of vorticity does not turn too fast where it is large, the solution stays smooth. Beirão da Veiga and Berselli lowered that cut to Hölder 1/2 in 2002. Those papers are theorems as ifs.

They do not estimate the pairs they refused: intense vorticity whose direction is misaligned. That refused set is the swirl leftover. I name the missing bound WRITE (6). The estimate is stated. It is not proved. A picture of a vortex that spirals in is not that estimate.

Magazine cut. About the leftover, dated, and not sold as a close. Full map: the swirl paper.
